# Hero Ability Zone

The Hero Ability Zone is where the [Hero Ability](../../../item-types/cards/hero-abilities.md) lives.

There can be only one Hero Ability in this zone at a time.

In most cases, the Hero Ability is associated with a [Prism Hero](../../../item-types/cards/heroes.md) and does not change.

## Special Cases

Banjo has a Hero Ability that replaces itself with another Hero Ability at the start of every turn.
