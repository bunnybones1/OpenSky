# Combat

Combat is when a Character from one side of the field is told to attack a Character on the other side.

Combat has an attacker and a defender.

Each combattant loses the amount of [Health](../../../item-types/cards/properties/health.md) equal to the other combattant's [Power](../../../item-types/cards/properties/power.md).

If a combattant is hurt by a combattant with the Wither trait, they also lose the amount of [Power](../../../item-types/cards/properties/power.md) equal to the other combattant's [Power](../../../item-types/cards/properties/power.md).

## Exceptions

Heroes do not lose [Power](../../../item-types/cards/properties/power.md) when they are hurt by a combattant with the wither trait.

Attackers who attack a Hero do not lose any [Health](../../../item-types/cards/properties/health.md).
