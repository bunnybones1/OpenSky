# Matches

A Match is a turn-based contest where two players bring a [deck of cards](../../item-types/decks.md) and a [hero](../../item-types/cards/heroes.md) to an arena, and take turns trying to reduce their opponent's health to zero.

A player the following at their disposal:

- a [hero](../../item-types/cards/heroes.md)
- a [mana vial](./mana-vial.md)
- their [deck of cards](../../item-types/decks.md)

Other vital concepts in a match include [zones](./zones/README.md), which exists for each player.

## Prism Playstyles

Each [Prism](../../../lore/prisms/README.md) is associated with a playstyle:

- Strength - Aggressive units
- Agility - Cheap Units and Spells, and a strong Hero
- Heart - Death effects
- Wisdom - Lots of Mana, and drawing cards
- Intelligence - Mulliganning your hand frequently
