# Summoning

Summoning is the act of adding a [Unit](../../item-types/cards/units.md) to the [field](../matches/zones/field.md).

It is usually done by paying the [mana cost](../../item-types/cards/properties/mana-cost.md) of a [Unit card](../../item-types/cards/units.md), but there are also [card effects](../../item-types/cards/properties/card-effects/README.md) that summon units without paying a mana cost.
