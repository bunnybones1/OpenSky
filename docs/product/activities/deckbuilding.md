# Deckbuilding

Deckbuilding is the activity of assembling a legal [deck](../item-types/decks.md) from a player's available cards.

## Role in the Product

Deckbuilding is where collection, strategy, and prism identity meet. It turns owned cards into a playable plan.

## What The Deck Builder Does

The deck builder helps players:

- choose legal cards for the intended hero or prism pairing
- review mana curve, roles, and synergies
- save decks for use in [constructed matches](./matches/constructed.md)
