# Seasons

A season is a 28 day period in OpenSky that has a time-limited theme and rewards on the skypass.

## Possible variations

Seasons will start on Monday morning EST, and rotate every 28 days

Seasons will start on the first Monday of each month, in the morning EST, and rotate every 28 or 25 days

Seasons will start on Monday morning EST, and rotate every 28 days

[Experience and Levels](./experience-and-levels.md) reset when the season resets.
