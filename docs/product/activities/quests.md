# Quests

Quests are rotating gameplay objectives that reward [Skypass experience](./skypass/experience-and-levels.md).

## Role in the Product

Quests create short- and medium-term goals beyond simply winning matches. They push players toward trying different cards, lines of play, or activity patterns.

## Structure

Players receive quests across multiple time horizons:

- daily quests
- weekly quests
- season quests

## Refresh and Rerolls

Each quest period refreshes on its own cadence. Players also receive reroll opportunities tied to those periods so that impossible or undesirable quests do not stall progression entirely.
