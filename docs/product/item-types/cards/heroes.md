# Heroes

The Hero is the most important [character card](./characters.md) type in OpenSky.

The Hero is used as the central character in [Matches](../../activities/matches/README.md) and [Deckbuilding](../../activities/deckbuilding.md).

## Prism Heroes

Prism Heroes are the main Hero type of OpenSky.

A Prism Hero can be mono or dual Prism.

A Hero's Prism dictates what cards are available in deckbuilding. The Prism also limits what cards can be conjured during a match.

They don't have an elemental affinity.
