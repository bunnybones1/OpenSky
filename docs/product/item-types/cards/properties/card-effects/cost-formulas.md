# Cost Formula

A card in the [deck](../../../../activities/matches/zones/deck.md) or [hand](../../../../activities/matches/zones/hand.md) can have a rule which affects its [mana cost](../mana-cost.md) dynamically.

## Examples

A card that costs 10 mana, but says `Costs 1 less for each Unit that died this turn`.

A card that says `Costs all your unspent mana`.
