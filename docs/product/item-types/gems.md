# Gems

Gems are the premium currency used for monetized purchases and higher-value progression transactions in OpenSky.

## Role in the Product

Gems sit on the paid side of the economy. They are used when the product wants a durable, account-level premium balance rather than a match reward or non-tradeable progression currency.

## Contrast With Other Value Types

- unlike [Spark](./spark.md), Gems are positioned as premium value rather than earned progression utility
- unlike collectible items, Gems are fungible and primarily transactional
- unlike rank or Skypass progress, Gems represent spending power rather than status
