# Card Backs

Cardbacks are cosmetics equipped on an account so that the player's [cards](./cards/README.md) display that design during a [match](../activities/matches/README.md).

## Role in the Product

Cardbacks are identity cosmetics. They let players personalize one of the most consistently visible surfaces in the game without affecting gameplay rules.

## Product Use

Because cardbacks are account-level cosmetics with high visibility, they fit well as prestige rewards, seasonal rewards, or premium cosmetic unlocks.
