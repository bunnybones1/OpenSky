# Stickers

Stickers are collectible cosmetic emotes that players can use during [matches](../activities/matches/README.md).

## Role in the Product

They do not change gameplay outcome. Their value is expressive and social: they let players react, taunt, celebrate, or signal personality during play.

## Distribution

Stickers are often used as reward-track or event-friendly cosmetics because they are easy to grant, easy to display, and low-risk from a balance perspective.
