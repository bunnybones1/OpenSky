# The Great Library of Alcazar

### The Greatest Repository of Knowledge in the Waking World

The Library of Alcazar is the magnum opus of the OpenSky Lotus and his followers. A dozen-storey high building covering 10+ acres of land, the library contains hundreds of millions of tomes, data records, scrolls and grimoires from all across Sky's history, ranging from the youngest children's books to forbidden magical scrolls (kept under countless locks and keys) holding spells capable of rendering entire continents asunder.

The library is guarded by a number of elite spellcasters and magical creatures who work to protect its sacred knowledge and prevent its misuse.

Though Alcazar contains an unfathomable amount of Knowledge, many hold that there is an even greater repository of Knowledge, in the form of the Masked Dreamer's Grand Archives at the Dreamfield's heart, which is said to contain a record of everything that has ever happened in Sky.
