# The Ashlands

### A massive desert-like crater filled with swirling ash and cinders

The Ashlands are a barren crater-biome where choking dust, cinders, and heat-scarred
winds make ordinary settlement difficult and often lethal. Little grows there. Survival
belongs to scavengers, ash-adapted beasts, hardened travelers, and the few human groups
willing to build lives in a place most of Sky would rather avoid.

Their reputation is part environmental and part mythic. Some treat the Ashlands as cursed,
a wound in the world where life was never meant to return. Others believe the region was
created by an ancient impact, perhaps from a meteor of toxic or unnatural metal. The
valuable shards sometimes found beneath the ash keep that theory alive.

Whatever its origin, the Ashlands feel like a place where old disasters never fully ended.
They are a natural home for scavenger stories, salvage journeys, pilgrimage-by-hardship, and any
narrative that needs a landscape shaped by survival rather than comfort.
