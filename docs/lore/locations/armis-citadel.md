# Armis Citadel

### The heart of the dominion of Armis

The Citadel of Armis sits at the centre of the Armis' territory - The Citadel is a fortress-city with hundred-foot tall walls encircling it, and serves as the living place of the Armis upper and military castes. The citadel is surrounded by swaths of countryside where the farmer caste of Armis works to feed the million+ strong standing military caste, which are represented in-game by Armored Guards, Honor Guard, Ghost Duster, and similar units. Armis is constantly seeking to expand the territory it holds, believing all of Sky is destined to be united under its banner. To the Armis, there are only two kinds of people in Sky. The Armis, and those who oppose them.

- Armis is a facist dictatorship with ideas of a corrupt communism where "some are more equal than others"
- If you follow the path of Armis and fight for their ideologies you're rewarded in kind. Insolence is not acceptable and will end up with you behind bars or worse.
- Power and weapons are the major commodities valued in Armis. Furthermore, technology's only value to the Armisian is it's ability to be used in converting citizens of Sky.
- Armis: Brutalist Architecture, polished silver metal.. Oppressive skyscrapers.
