# Kore City

### A meeting place for peoples from across Sky to live as one

Kore City is the clearest urban crossroads in Sky: a place where trade, invention, study,
and migration pull together peoples who would otherwise remain divided by distance,
custom, or old suspicion.

Its streets and institutions are built on diversity as a practical reality rather than an ideal
slogan. Lapin, Revenants, humans, Tortugan, Leorans, Primalans, and even harder-edged
outsiders such as Oni, Exo, and the occasional Wraith can all be found there, living in a
peace that is real but never effortless.

That density of cultures, skills, and competing ambitions has made Kore City both a major
center of learning and one of Sky's fastest-moving hubs of technical progress. Universities,
colleges, workshops, research facilities, and experimental arenas all thrive there, giving the
city a reputation as the place where ideas are tested against one another in public.
