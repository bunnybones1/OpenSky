# The Void

### The all-consuming abyss at the deepest point in the Undercroft

At the deepest known point of the Undercroft lies the Void: an abyss so absolute that even
its witnesses cannot agree on what they have seen. Some claim the darkness contains the
faces of the dead. Others see screaming skulls, or hear the pull of lost loved ones calling
from below. Still others see nothing at all beyond depth, wind, and the certainty that they
should not remain there.

That uncertainty is part of the Void's terror. It may reflect the mind of the observer, expose
buried fear, or reveal something genuinely waiting inside. What matters more to the rest of
Sky is the one detail on which every account agrees: nothing that enters the Void ever
returns.

The Wraiths claim a special kinship with this abyss, and many people believe they are born
from it or shaped by its influence. Whether that is true or merely one more fear the Wraiths
encourage, the claim has helped make the Void one of the most dreaded places in the
setting.
