# Mavron, River of Tears

Mavron is a myth-laden river of the Undercroft, bound to death, passage, and the emotional geography of the subterranean world.

## Role in the Setting

- It functions as both a physical river and a symbolic threshold.
- It reinforces the Undercroft's association with burial, memory, and the afterlife.
- It helps make the lower world feel spiritually inhabited rather than merely dark or hostile.
