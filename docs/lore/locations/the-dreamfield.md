# The Dreamfield

The Dreamfield is a parallel realm of dreams, thought, and distortion that overlaps with Sky without obeying its ordinary laws.

## Nature of the Realm

The Dreamfield exists beside waking reality rather than beneath it or beyond it in a conventional geographic sense. Time, distance, and causality all behave unreliably there. A traveler might cross vast stretches of Sky in minutes through the Dreamfield, or spend what feels like hours inside it only to discover that almost no time has passed outside - or far too much has.

## Inhabitants and Dangers

The Dreamfield is populated by beings shaped more by thought and emotion than by stable biology. Dreambeasts, Animata, and other unstable entities thrive there because the realm rewards transformation, obsession, and psychic intensity over fixed form.

It is also haunted by a deeper threat: Yxxathoxzz, the sleeping devourer at its center. Many stories about the Dreamfield ultimately reduce to one question: what happens if that sleep fails?

## Connections to Sky

The Dreamfield is not sealed off from the waking world. Ancient portals can open onto it, and some individuals claim to reach it through dreams, trance, or specialized ritual practice. Those crossings make it strategically valuable and spiritually dangerous at the same time.

## Role in the Setting

The Dreamfield gives Sky a realm where memory, fear, knowledge, and distance can all become unstable. It is useful as a source of wonder, but it matters most as a source of narrative pressure: secret travel, occult knowledge, warped creatures, and the constant possibility that thought itself may become predatory.
