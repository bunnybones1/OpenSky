# The Etherwald

The Etherwald is the vast sacred forest most closely associated with Ethera, the World Tree,
and the living balance of Sky.

## Character of the Forest

The Etherwald is not merely large. It is civilizationally and cosmologically important. Its elder
woods, crystal growth, and living systems make it feel less like a biome and more like one of
Sky's foundational organs. The forest is often described as inseparable from the world's wider
health: if the Etherwald were truly ruined, the damage would not remain local.

## Ether Crystals and the World Tree

Ether Crystals grow through the forest in symbiotic relationship with its plant life, binding the
region's ecology to one of the setting's most important magical resources. At the forest's heart
stands the World Tree, a primordial living structure whose condition is tied to the stability of the
Etherwald as a whole.

## Guardians and Inhabitants

The Etherwald is watched over by Dryads, Treefolk, beasts, and other life bound to its cycles.
Amaruath, the Earth Dragon, is also closely associated with the forest and with the kind of
ancient custodianship the region demands.

## Role in the Setting

The Etherwald concentrates many of Sky's key tensions in one place: ecological interdependence,
resource extraction, sacred stewardship, and the danger of treating living systems as fuel. It is
one of the clearest places where environmental harm becomes political, spiritual, and existential
at once.
