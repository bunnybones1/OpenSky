# Locations

Important cities, realms, and mythic regions across Sky.

## Cities and Strongholds

- [Armis Citadel](./armis-citadel.md): The fortress-capital of Armis and symbolic center of its expansionist military order.
- [Kore City](./kore-city.md): A diverse supercity of trade, invention, and uneasy coexistence among Sky's peoples.
- [The Grand Arena](./the-grand-arena.md): A civic spectacle space where commerce, competition, and public glory converge.
- [The Great Library of Alcazar](./the-great-library-of-alcazar.md): Lotus's immense repository of knowledge, lore, and forbidden magical records.

## Regions and Frontiers

- [The Ashlands](./the-ashlands.md): A barren crater-wasteland of ash, scavengers, and rumors of something ancient buried below.
- [The Etherwald](./the-etherwald.md): A vast sacred forest tied to Ethera, the World Tree, and the living balance of Sky.
- [The Undercroft](./the-undercroft.md): The connected cave-world beneath Sky where ruins, portals, and dark-aligned peoples endure.

## Mythic and Liminal Places

- [Mavron, River of Tears](./mavron-river-of-tears.md): A myth-laden river of the Undercroft tied to death, afterlife beliefs, and subterranean survival.
- [The Dreamfield](./the-dreamfield.md): A distorted dream-realm where time, space, and thought no longer obey waking logic.
- [The Void](./the-void.md): An abyss beneath the Undercroft from which nothing returns and onto which countless fears are projected.
