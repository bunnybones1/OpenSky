# The Undercroft

### A vast System Caverns that lies Beneath the Surface of Sky

The Undercroft is the name for the systems of caves, tunnels and ruins that honeycombs the earth and stone beneath the islands of Sky. Though the floating islands that make up Sky are separate, a system of ancient portals connects them within the Undercroft, allowing the vast cave systems of the Undercroft to exist more or less as a giant continual space.

The Undercroft is home to many of Sky's Dark-Aligned creatures, with the Exo, and Revenants making up the bulk of the population. Wraiths, Demons, and Fallen Raptors are also found within it, using the dark for their hunting grounds and hiding places.

The Undercroft also holds untold secrets amongst its ancient ruins, typically well guarded by deadly traps and monsters, and the foolish sometimes venture into them, seeking fame and fortune, though few ever return.
