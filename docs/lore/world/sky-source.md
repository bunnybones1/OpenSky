# Sky Source

The Sky Source is the immense energy mass at the center of Sky. The world's floating lands revolve around it, and many people in the setting regard it as the origin of islands, Prisms, and matter itself.

## Role in the Setting

- It is the cosmological center of Sky rather than merely an important place within it.
- It anchors the world's magic, geography, and many of its creation myths.
- New islands are still said to emerge from it on rare occasions.

## Why It Matters

The Sky Source is not only a source of power. It is also the standard against which many people measure legitimacy, destiny, and divine order. Any struggle over Prisms, OpenSkys, or world-shaping artifacts eventually becomes a struggle over humanity's relationship to the Sky Source.
