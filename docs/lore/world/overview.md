# Sky

Sky is a dimension formed from the convergence of multiple realities. Its floating lands revolve around the Sky Source, an immense concentration of power at the world's center.

Across the ages, Prisms have chosen champions known as OpenSkys. These champions channel the power of the Sky Source to summon creatures, cast spells, protect their people, and impose their ideals on the world around them.

## Core Concepts

- `Sky Source`: The central energy mass from which islands, Prisms, and much of Sky's power are believed to arise.
- `Prisms`: Ancient artifacts that focus distinct aspects of the Sky Source and align their wielders with particular ideals.
- `OpenSkys`: Practitioners who learn to channel the Sky Source through Prisms.
- `Heroes`: Exceptional OpenSkys who have mastered a Prism or a combination of Prisms.
- `Genesis Prism`: A legendary Prism said to grant access to the full power of the Sky Source.

## The Current Era

The modern age is defined by instability. OpenSkys, rulers, and rival factions are pulled into open struggle by the possibility of the Genesis Prism and by the spread of world-altering threats such as the Glitch Storm and the Hex Plague.

The setting's central question is not simply who can gather power, but what kind of order will survive if they do. For the active pressures shaping that struggle, see [Conflicts](./conflicts.md).
