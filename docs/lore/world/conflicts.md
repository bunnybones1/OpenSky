# Conflicts

These are the active setting-level pressures shaping the current age of Sky.

## The Genesis Prism Race

The Genesis Prism is the setting's most dangerous prize. OpenSkys and rulers pursue it for incompatible reasons: to preserve balance, defend their people, defeat enemies, or reshape Sky according to their own ideals.

## The Glitch Storm

The Glitch Storm distorts creatures, landscapes, and structures into unstable new forms. It creates immediate disaster, but it also destabilizes memory, territory, and the reliability of the world itself.

## The Hex Plague

Corrupted Ether Crystals have given rise to the Hex Plague and the Hexbound. What begins as infection becomes expansion, turning a technical catastrophe into a civilizational threat.

## Ether Crystal Extraction

Ether Crystals are indispensable enough to drive exploitation, theft, and ecological harm. That pressure falls especially hard on places like the Etherwald, where extraction becomes both an economic and spiritual conflict.

## Dreamfield Pressure

The Dreamfield remains one of the least stable frontiers in Sky. Its influence produces warped beings, unreliable boundaries, and threats such as Yxxathoxzz, whose continued sleep depends on a fragile and morally dubious balance.
