# Timeline

Major historical milestones across Sky.

## Known Chronology

- `1 billion years BD`: The Eight Dragons appear.
- `80 million years BD`: The Great Glitch Storm reshapes Sky and is credited as an origin point for many races.
- `370`: Armis City is founded.
- `2554`: Some Ether Crystals become corrupted, giving rise to the Hex.
- `3400`: Present-day reference point in the current timeline notes.

## Unresolved Historical Anchors

The current lore material still points to several important but underdefined milestones:

- `22 million years BD`
- `90 thousand years BD`
- `Year Zero`, possibly tied to the birth of the OpenSkys
- `495`, with an incomplete Arcadia note
- `964`
- `1024`
- `1355`
- `2120`
- `3044`

These remain part of the timeline's structure, but they are not developed enough yet to present as full historical entries.
