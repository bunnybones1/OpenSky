# Core Terms

## Sky

Sky is a dimension assembled from multiple realities. Its islands orbit the Sky Source rather than resting on a single continuous world.

## OpenSkys

OpenSkys are people who learn to channel the power of the Sky Source through Prisms.

## Heroes

Heroes are OpenSkys who have mastered a specific Prism or a specific Prism combination.

## Prisms

Prisms are rare ethereal artifacts that focus distinct aspects of the Sky Source's power. Each Prism also carries a philosophical identity that shapes the duties and temptations of its wielders.

## Weaving

Weaving is the act of manipulating the Sky Source's energy through summons, spells, and other forms of magical shaping.

## Genesis Prism

The Genesis Prism is the legendary Prism said to channel the full power of the Sky Source with no ordinary constraints.

## Glitch Storm

The Glitch Storm is a reality-warping force that distorts whatever it touches and has become one of the defining dangers of the current age.

## Hex Plague

The Hex Plague is a crystal-born corruption that spreads through living systems and produces the Hexbound.

## Dreamfield

The Dreamfield is a distorted realm where time, space, and thought no longer behave according to waking logic.
