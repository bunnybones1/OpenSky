# Wu-Kin

![Wu-Kin](../images/races/Wu-Kin.png)

The Wu-Kin are monkey-like guardians of ancient ruins who believe some knowledge is too
dangerous to be freely unearthed.

### Culture and Purpose

- The Wu-Kin believe the buried secrets of Sky are dangerous precisely because they are powerful.
- Their duty is not discovery for its own sake, but protection, concealment, and refusal.
- They live near ruins, temples, and sealed vaults, often in small guardian groups assigned to specific sites.
- Some remain faithful to that vigil for life. Others leave it behind to explore Sky on their own terms.

### Combat and Magic

- They are strongly tied to air magic, mobility, and cloud-riding movement.
- Their use of vault keys and ancestral weapons reinforces the idea that guardianship is built directly into their martial identity.

### Place in the Setting

- The Wu-Kin give Sky a faction defined by curatorship rather than conquest.
- They embody one of the setting's recurring themes: that knowledge can save a civilization or destroy it, depending on who reaches it first.
