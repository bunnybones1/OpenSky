# Vulpine

![Vulpine](../images/races/Vulpine.png)

The Vulpine are fox-like tricksters, duelists, and illusionists whose culture blends
mischief, pride, and a surprisingly strict sense of honor.

### Physical Characteristics

- Fox-like people typically marked by reddish-orange fur and pale underbellies.
- Tail count matters symbolically, with multiple tails associated with unusual magical promise.
- Their clothing and equipment tend to support mobility, travel, stealth, or theatrical self-presentation.

### Common Traits

- Clever, smug, competitive, and rarely immune to the appeal of a good prank.
- Strongly individualistic, but still shaped by communal reputation and honor.
- More comfortable with trickery and indirection than with blunt domination.
- Prone to rationalizing theft-adjacent behavior so long as it does not violate their own codes too openly.

### Culture and Society

Vulpine culture is built around performance, wit, rivalry, and style. Outsiders often meet
them first as pranksters or hustlers, but the source suggests something more refined beneath
that surface: hidden settlements, internal standards of conduct, and a social world that
values cunning without equating it with lawlessness. They enjoy deception as craft, not
merely as predation.

### Technology and Advancements

The Vulpine lean more toward magic, swordsmanship, and personal skill than toward large
technological systems. That does not make them anti-technology; it simply is not the main
axis of their cultural identity.

### Homelands and Environment

They tend to inhabit mountain foothills and nearby forests, places well suited to concealment,
travel, and illusion work. Secret Vulpine dens function as
hideouts, communal support hubs, and points of contact for more clandestine operations.

### Political Structure

The Vulpine do not read as a centralized state. Instead, status appears to come from skill,
reputation, and symbolic signs of distinction. The goddess Vulpis serves as a major cultural
figure, and multi-tailed Vulpine are treated as prodigies whose power carries social weight.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

The Vulpine are worldly enough to interact with many peoples, but they are especially linked
to the Wolfkin and the Oni. Their rivalry with the Wolfkin is one of the clearest recurring
threads in their lore: part competition, part mutual fascination. Many Vulpine also wander
Sky in pursuit of stories, mastery, or opportunities worthy of their talents.

### Magic or Special Abilities

The Vulpine are especially associated with illusion, disguise, fire magic, and agile martial
craft. Their talents make them natural rogues, duelists, wandering artisans, and magical
deceivers, even if no single role defines the whole people.
