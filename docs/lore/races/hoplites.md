# Hoplites

![Hoplites](../images/races/Hoplites.png)

The Hoplites are disciplined shield-warriors whose culture treats civic duty, formation,
and sacrifice as the foundation of legitimate power.

### Physical Characteristics

- Broad-shouldered humanoids most often depicted in heavy bronze or iron panoply.
- Strong visual identity built around shields, spears, crested helms, and tightly ordered ranks.
- Their battlefield appearance emphasizes unity over individual flourish.

### Common Traits

- Stoic, dutiful, and suspicious of anyone who treats warfare as spectacle alone.
- Trained to think in terms of line, formation, and collective responsibility.
- Proud of discipline, but vulnerable to rigidity when circumstances demand improvisation.

### Culture and Society

Hoplite culture is built around the idea that a people survives only when each person holds
the line. Civic life, military service, and public honor therefore blur together. A Hoplite is
judged not only by courage in battle, but by whether they can keep faith with the shield-wall,
with the city that armed them, and with the comrades whose lives depend on them. That gives
their society admirable cohesion, but it can also make them harsh toward dissenters, drifters,
and anyone who values personal freedom above public obligation.

### Technology and Advancements

Their greatest refinements are martial rather than industrial. Hoplite armor, shieldcraft,
fortification, and field logistics are all shaped by the demands of disciplined mass warfare.
They excel at building systems that let ordinary soldiers hold against superior monsters,
raiders, or magic-heavy enemies through preparation and formation.

### Homelands and Environment

Hoplite settlements are associated with defensible stone cities, high terraces, and exposed
frontiers where organized defense matters more than luxury. Their landscapes tend to feel
fortified even in peacetime: roads, walls, signal towers, and drill grounds all reinforce the
sense of a people prepared to be attacked.

### Political Structure

Hoplite states are usually governed through a mix of magistrates, commanders, and civic
councils rather than through purely personal rule. Even powerful leaders are expected to
justify authority through service to the whole, not merely through bloodline or charisma.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

They respect peoples who can maintain order under pressure, even if they dislike them. That
creates cautious admiration for disciplined cultures like Armis and the Oni, but also deep
hostility whenever those powers mistake domination for stewardship. To more fluid or chaotic
cultures, the Hoplites often appear honorable, humorless, and exhausting.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Hoplite strength lies less in exotic magic than in coordinated warfare, battlefield discipline,
and protective formations that let ordinary courage become something close to ritual power.
