# Elementals

![Elementals](../images/races/Elementals.png)

Elementals are living manifestations of elemental force whose behavior reflects the state of
the environments from which they emerge.

### Nature

- Elementals are less a people than expressions of elemental conditions made animate.
- They are not inherently benevolent or hostile. Their temperament is tied to the health, imbalance, or corruption of their surroundings.
- Because of that, they can function as indicators of ecological truth: when an environment is wounded, its elementals may be as well.

### Setting Use

- Elementals reinforce the idea that Sky's magic is environmental, not abstract.
- They help link ecology and conflict, showing that elemental disturbance has living consequences.
- Even brief references to them broaden the sense that every biome in Sky has a spiritual and reactive dimension.
