# Rashidans

![Rashidans](../images/races/Rashidans.png)

The Rashidans are the central human civilization of the great desert kingdom of Rashida,
defined by solar devotion, sacred kingship, and a political order in which the living and the
dead remain bound to one another.

### Physical Characteristics

- Human desert-dwellers whose visual culture is shaped by gold, lapis, linen, obsidian, and sun motifs.
- Their clothing and regalia signal rank, priestly office, or relation to the sacred order of Rashida.
- Even ordinary Rashidan design tends to project refinement, ritual care, and durability under harsh light.

### Common Traits

- Deeply shaped by reverence for Kha Meth and the ordering power of light.
- Formal, proud, and attentive to lineage, memory, and rightful place.
- Capable of great hospitality within the bounds of ritual respect, and great severity when those bounds are broken.

### Culture and Society

Rashidan civilization is built on continuity. The kingdom does not treat the past as dead
matter to be superseded, but as an active source of legitimacy and obligation. Temples,
tombs, royal lines, the Net'jer, and the Royal Mummies all reinforce the same principle:
rulership and stewardship continue across generations rather than being severed by death.
That gives Rashida extraordinary cultural coherence. It also makes the kingdom highly
protective of sacred custom and deeply suspicious of powers that treat knowledge, burial,
or divine order as resources to be exploited.

### Technology and Advancements

Rashidan advancement is refined rather than industrial. Its strengths lie in monumental
architecture, funerary craft, solar theology, sacred record-keeping, and the magical sciences
needed to sustain a kingdom where ritual, rulership, and the afterlife remain politically real.

### Homelands and Environment

The Rashidans are the dominant people of Rashida's deserts, temple-cities, river settlements,
and burial complexes. Their civilization turns an unforgiving landscape into a place of order,
ceremony, and enduring state power.

### Political Structure

Rashida is a sacred monarchy supported by priesthoods, dynastic institutions, and the living
continuity of its ancestral dead. The ruler's authority is political, religious, and historical at
once. A Rashidan kingdom is expected not merely to govern the living, but to remain worthy
of the dead who still guard it.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

The Rashidans sit at the center of a broader desert order that includes the Net'jer, the Royal
Mummies, and allied peoples such as the Surit. They can be generous patrons and stable
neighbors, but they are not neutral cosmopolitans. Their worldview expects reverence toward
Rashida's sacred order, and they respond poorly to desecration, theft, or casual irreverence.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Rashidan magic is strongly aligned with light, preservation, burial rite, and sacred authority.
In Rashida, magic is not separate from government or religion. It is one of the main ways the
kingdom binds power, memory, and legitimacy together.
