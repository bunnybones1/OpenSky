# Dreambeasts

![Dreambeasts](../images/races/Dreambeasts.png)

Dreambeasts are mutable entities of the Dreamfield, born from dreams, nightmares,
thoughts, and emotion. They are among the most unpredictable beings in Sky: some are
intelligent enough to bargain with, others are little more than predatory instincts given
shape.

### Physical Characteristics

- Capable of taking almost any form.
- Can appear ethereal, physical, beautiful, absurd, or horrifying.
- Some are marked by cosmic or star-like skin, reinforcing their unreal origin.

### Common Traits

- Feed on dreams, thoughts, and emotions.
- Highly unstable in form and behavior.
- Dangerous regardless of whether they are clever, feral, or seemingly calm.

### Culture and Society

Dreambeasts do not appear to form a stable civilization. They are individualistic beings,
often driven by the emotional or conceptual substance from which they emerged. Some
pursue fear, some curiosity, some obsession, and some hunger without any larger social
purpose.

### Geography and Environment they Inhabit

They are most strongly tied to the Dreamfield, but they are not necessarily confined to it.
Dreambeasts can drift into Sky itself, especially when pursuing
interesting minds or especially potent emotions.

### Political Structure

There is no true Dreambeast polity. At most, weaker Dreambeasts may trail stronger ones
that are better at finding rich psychic prey.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Most Dreambeasts do not relate to other peoples politically or culturally. Other beings are
usually food, fascination, or both. They also show a strange recurring interest in the
Tortugan, though it does not fully explain why.

### Magic or Special Abilities

- Shapeshifting on a near-limitless scale.
- Dream- and mind-related predation.
- Reality-bending behavior not fully constrained by physical law.
