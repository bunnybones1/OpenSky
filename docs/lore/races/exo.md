# Exo

![Exo](../images/races/Exo.png)

The Exo are a feared insect-people of the Undercroft, shaped by exile, paranoia, and a
social order in which survival often outranks trust.

### Physical Characteristics

- Insectoid humanoids with wide visual variation across bug-like forms.
- Often defined by sharp sensory features, hard natural weapons, and an unsettling physical presence.
- Built for survival in darkness, confinement, and subterranean danger.

### Common Traits

- Suspicious of outsiders and often suspicious of one another as well.
- Pragmatic, dangerous, and shaped by grievance.
- Closely associated with stealth, subterfuge, and retaliatory violence.

### Culture and Society

The Exo are defined by banishment and consequence. Long ago they were driven
into the Undercroft for sins no one now clearly remembers, and that exile appears to have
reshaped the species from the inside. Exo society is not stable in the civic sense; it is
competitive, fragmented, and saturated with power struggles. Some Exo want only to survive.
Others want revenge on the world above that cast them down.

### Technology and Advancements

The Exo adapt rather than idealize. Their tools, weapons, and techniques are designed for
darkness, infiltration, ambush, and quick killing. What they build is less about civilizational
display and more about the practical demands of living in a hostile subterranean world.

### Homelands and Environment

They belong to the Undercroft: tunnels, caverns, choke points, and shadowed territories
where visibility is limited and every movement can be contested. Territoriality is not just
instinct for them; it is one of the conditions of survival.

### Political Structure

The Exo are not presented as a unified nation. Power is local, unstable, and often won
through treachery. Leadership changes by assassination, maneuver, or collapse, leaving
their politics fractured into competing cells and factions rather than a shared state.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Other peoples tend to fear the Exo, often for good reason. Exo moving above ground are
commonly framed as mercenaries, spies, thieves, or assassins. They can cooperate with
other dark-aligned peoples such as Wraiths or Revenants when interests overlap, but the
setting does not imply deep trust even there.

### Magic or Special Abilities

The Exo excel at stealth, ambush, and survival in the dark. Their senses, speed, and natural
weapons make them formidable predators underground. One of the most important setting
details attached to them is immunity to the Crystal Plague, which distinguishes them sharply
from many other peoples of Sky.

### Additional Details

- Those outside the Undercroft work as mercenaries, spies, or thieves.
- Many Exo secretly want more than vengeance: they want lives that are safer,
  freer, and no longer confined to darkness.
