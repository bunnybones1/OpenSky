# Oni

![Oni](../images/races/Oni.png)

The Oni are mountain warrior-clans defined by strength, hierarchy, craft, and a severe code
of honor that can harden easily into tyranny.

### Physical Characteristics

- Red-skinned humanoids marked by horns, fangs, pointed ears, and imposing physiques.
- Strong visual identity shaped by armor, weapons, and clan presentation.
- Their appearance reinforces the source's repeated link between the Oni and demonic ancestry.

### Common Traits

- Hardy in extreme mountain climates, whether volcanic or frozen.
- Suspicious of outsiders unless martial worth has been proven.
- Deeply invested in combat as art, law, and social ordering principle.
- Proud, severe, and often quick to anger when honor is challenged.

### Culture and Society

Oni society is organized around close-knit clans or families ruled by Shoguns. Strength,
discipline, and martial excellence are treated as social virtues, not merely battlefield
skills. That gives their culture an undeniable dignity, but also a brutal edge. Weakness can
be punished harshly, and honor codes that sound noble from a distance may operate as tools
of exclusion, coercion, or violence within the clan.

### Technology and Advancements

The Oni are master smiths as much as they are warriors. Their weapons and armor are tied
to status, discipline, and clan pride, and poor workmanship is treated almost as moral
failure. Craftsmanship is therefore one of the main ways Oni culture turns violence into
ritual and prestige.

### Homelands and Environment

They inhabit the harshest mountain environments in Sky, from volcanic heights to frozen
ridges. Their strongholds are often placed high above surrounding lands, reinforcing both
their strategic advantage and their cultural belief that power ought to stand above others.

### Political Structure

Oni politics are hierarchical and often harsh. Shoguns rule with near-absolute authority
within their domains, while artisans and elite warriors gain high standing beneath them.
Because each ruling house prizes superiority, the broader Oni world is prone to rivalry,
feud, and shifting alliance rather than stable unity.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

The Oni distrust outsiders by default, but their loyalty can be fierce once earned. They are
most legible to peoples who also prize prowess, rivalry, and personal valor. They are
especially linked with the Vulpine and Wolfkin, who sometimes fight beside them and
sometimes test themselves against them.

### Additional Details

- Oni tradition also preserves a demonic heritage and memories of an older Devilkin-Oni War.
- Oni Firewine is described as famously potent and culturally iconic.
- Commanders are said to set banners ablaze before battle, turning an advance into a ritual
  display of terror and pride.
