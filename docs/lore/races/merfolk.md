# Merfolk

An ancient race of half-human, half-fish guardians of the oceans.

### Physical Characteristics

- Humanoid above the waist and fish-tailed below, with a classic oceanic silhouette.
- Often marked by bright tails, webbed hands, gills, and other features suited to deep-water life.
- Graceful in motion and visually tied to the elegance of the sea itself.

### Common Traits

- Strong elemental affinity for water.
- Highly adapted to ocean depths and underwater navigation.
- Protective of marine ecosystems and of the sacred knowledge housed in their cities.
- Reclusive, but not isolationist by nature.

### Culture and Society

The Merfolk are presented as one of Sky's oldest custodial peoples, responsible not only
for living in the oceans but for guarding them. Their culture values knowledge, continuity,
and reverence for marine life. They are not simply fish-tailed counterparts to surface
societies; they function more like the sea's archivists, healers, and wardens.

### Technology and Advancements

The Merfolk practice a refined underwater craft tradition centered on gems, coral, rare sea
materials, medicine, and sacred artifacts. Their advances are not industrial in the Armis or
Tortugan sense, but they are sophisticated and specialized in ways surface peoples often
underestimate.

### Homelands and Environment

They live in hidden underwater cities built among reefs, caverns, vents, and other natural
formations of the deep. These cities are not just settlements but sanctuaries. Their temples
store knowledge and artifacts tied to the defense of the sea and to the broader health of
marine ecosystems.

### Political Structure

Merfolk leadership centers on Queen Encantadora and a council of elder
representatives tied to different cities. Governance appears orderly, role-based, and rooted
in stewardship. Individuals are often assigned or guided into roles that support communal
and ecological stability, though the system does not read as cruelly rigid.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

The Merfolk are cautious with outsiders because much of what they protect should not be
easily accessed. Still, they are capable of alliance and mutual respect, especially with peoples
who treat the environment as something to care for rather than consume. Their clearest
hostility in the source is directed toward Armis for destructive extraction, while the Tortugan
earn more goodwill through reciprocity and ecological responsibility.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Merfolk wield water magic as both craft and guardianship. They can manipulate currents,
shape underwater defenses, communicate with marine life, and monitor the health of the
oceans through more than ordinary observation. Their efforts
to repair damage caused by Armis expansion may eventually push sea and land into open
political conflict.
