# Saurian

![Saurian](../images/races/Saurian.png)

The Saurian are reptilian survivalists of Sky's harsher frontiers, known for their patience,
physical resilience, and a social logic shaped by territory, climate, and long memory.

### Physical Characteristics

- Reptilian humanoids with scales, tails, and visual variation across draconic or lizard-like lineages.
- Built for heat tolerance, harsh terrain, and sudden bursts of violence when threatened.
- Their armor and ornament often emphasize hide, bone, stone, and trophies taken from dangerous environments.

### Common Traits

- Watchful, territorial, and far less impulsive than outsiders first assume.
- Strongly oriented toward survival, hierarchy, and practical competence.
- Capable of stillness and patience that can read as coldness to more openly social peoples.

### Culture and Society

Saurian culture is shaped by the conviction that weakness is expensive and memory is a form
of armor. Their communities appear to value caution, inherited environmental knowledge, and
a hard-earned respect for whatever keeps a settlement alive. That does not make them joyless.
It makes them difficult to impress with anything that cannot endure heat, hunger, predation,
or time. Their outlook is often misread as primitive by more decorative civilizations, when it
is better understood as austere competence developed under pressure.

### Technology and Advancements

The Saurian do not read as an industrial power, but neither are they unsophisticated. Their
craft is adapted to survival: resilient weapons, durable shelter, field medicine, heat-weathered
construction, and the tools needed to dominate dangerous ground rather than to beautify it.

### Homelands and Environment

They are most at home in badlands, volcanic margins, harsh jungle edges, and other regions
where heat, rock, and predation eliminate the careless. Saurian settlements tend to merge with
the terrain, using elevation, concealment, and natural barriers as extensions of defense.

### Political Structure

Saurian leadership seems to follow proven strength, continuity of lineage, and command of the
land rather than abstract claims of nobility. A ruler who cannot defend territory or preserve the
conditions of survival will not keep loyalty for long.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

The Saurian are cautious with outsiders because most visitors arrive wanting something:
passage, territory, trophies, or proof of dominance. They respect peoples who understand harsh
landscapes and despise those who mistake endurance for backwardness. That makes them more
likely to understand frontier peoples than empires.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Their strengths are tied to durability, ambush, and environmental adaptation. When Saurian
magic appears, it tends to feel elemental and practical - heat, stone, concealment, and the kind
of power that makes a body harder to move or kill.
