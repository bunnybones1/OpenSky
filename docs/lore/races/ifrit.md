# Ifrit

The Ifrit are an ascetic fire tradition associated with Oni mystics and mountain orders that
treat flame as discipline rather than indulgence.

### Philosophy

- The Ifrit path frames fire as purification, ordeal, and self-command.
- Its teachings value sacrifice, endurance, and the kind of clarity that can only be earned through hardship.
- The tradition survives in mountain shrines, isolated practitioners, and transmitted sayings rather than in a large public institution.

### Sayings

- "Oni Ascetics of the mountain ranges of Sky..."
- "Relinquish all to fire..."

### Role In Lore

- The Ifrit broaden the Oni beyond brute militarism by preserving a spiritual fire discipline within their world.
- They suggest that flame in Sky can signify inward purification as much as outward destruction.
