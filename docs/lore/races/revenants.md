# Revenants

![Revenants](../images/races/Revenants.png)

A highly organized, civilized race of undead humanoids that populate the Undercroft.

### Common Traits

- Very similar to humans, but lacking mortal weaknesses.
- Do not need to eat, breathe, or drink in a traditional sense (but might do it for pleasure).
- Sustained by absorbing the dark energies of the Undercroft.
- Form families, governments, and cities of their own.
- Distrusted by 'light dwellers' and are thus distrustful of them in turn.
- Are reasonable, decent people, though they are often seen as cold.
- The harshness of their environment drives them to be pragmatic.
- Sometimes ally with the Exo and Wraiths.
- Can venture beyond the Undercroft, but will waste away in time.
- Where some of the first beings infected by the Crystal Plague.
- Unfairly blamed for it by some people, like the paranoid Armis.

### Culture and Society

Revenants are one of the clearest examples in the setting that undeath does not equal
madness or monstrosity. They are depicted as organized, urban, and socially functional:
people with families, governments, obligations, and a durable civic life shaped by the
Undercroft rather than by nostalgia for the surface.

### Homelands and Environment

Their civilization is rooted in the Undercroft, where darkness, subterranean travel, and
harsh conditions have forced a pragmatic political culture. Revenants can leave for a time,
but the source suggests that life away from the Undercroft eventually weakens them.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Surface societies often treat Revenants with fear or suspicion simply because they are
undead. That suspicion appears to be mutual. They can cooperate with other dark-aligned
peoples such as the Exo and Wraiths when interests align, but they are not presented as a
single unified bloc with them.
