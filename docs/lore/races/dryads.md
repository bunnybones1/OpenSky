# Dryads

![Dryads](../images/races/Dryads.png)

The Dryads are Ethera's daughters and among the oldest protectors of the Etherwald:
beautiful, severe, and utterly committed to the living order of the forest.

### Physical Characteristics

- Plant-touched humanoids often marked by green skin, long ears, and an unmistakably organic beauty.
- Clothing, weapons, and ornament are commonly grown, woven, or shaped from living material.
- Individual Dryads often seem visually tied to a particular plant type or floral identity.

### Common Traits

- Strong affinity for earth-aligned magic and forest stewardship.
- Capable of touch-based telepathic communion, especially with Treefolk.
- Protective, watchful, and often deeply suspicious of intruders.
- Fiercely caring toward the lives and ecosystems placed under their guard.

### Culture and Society

Dryad culture is inseparable from purpose. They are not a people who merely happen to
live in the Etherwald; they exist to defend it.
That gives them a clear moral center and a potentially harsh edge. Their beauty, grace, and
care for life are genuine, but so is their readiness to treat violation of the forest as a
spiritual offense rather than a simple trespass.

### Technology and Advancements

The Dryads do not pursue technology in an industrial sense. Their craft is botanical,
magical, and symbiotic: they shape living materials into clothing, structures, and weapons
without breaking from the logic of the forest that sustains them.

### Homelands and Environment

They are native above all to the Etherwald, the immense sacred forest bound to Ethera.
Within it, Dryads live among Elderwood groves, Ether Crystal networks, Treefolk, beasts,
and other forms of life that together make the forest feel more like a living polity than a
mere biome.

### Political Structure

The Dryads do not appear to operate through rigid internal hierarchy. They regard one
another more as equals under a shared sacred duty. Their final loyalty is not to a monarch
or council but to Ethera herself, whose authority gives moral shape to everything else.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

The Dryads are wary of outsiders by default. Respect for Ethera and for the laws of the
forest can earn tolerance, but violation can provoke severe punishment. The idea
of being "Joined" to the forest suggests that Dryad justice can be transformative, mystical,
and terrifying. Their closest relationship is with the Treefolk, whom they help guide and
with whom they can commune directly.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Dryads wield earth magic, touch telepathy, and a deep command of living plant matter.
They are among the most natural defenders the Etherwald could produce. It also
suggests that while they can leave the forest for limited periods, prolonged separation from
it is spiritually and physically costly.
