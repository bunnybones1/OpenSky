# Armis

![Armis](../images/races/Armis.png)

Armis is a militarized human empire built on hierarchy, discipline, and the conviction
that order must be imposed on Sky by force if necessary.

### Physical Characteristics

- Human soldiers, nobles, and workers marked more by uniform and rank than by bloodline.
- Higher ranks wear heavier armor, richer gold ornament, and stronger visual signs of state authority.
- Arms and armor often carry a cold blue glow tied to Armis metalworking and battlefield tech.

### Common Traits

- Deeply conditioned to believe each person has a fixed role in society.
- Intolerant of disorder, dissent, or customs that fall outside state control.
- Proud of strength, discipline, and visible competence.
- Technologically advanced compared with many of Sky's other human powers.

### Culture and Society

Armis society treats the state as a machine and its people as components within it.
Strength, efficiency, and obedience are treated as virtues. From birth, citizens are pushed
toward roles that serve the larger order, and deviation is treated not as self-expression
but as a threat to stability. That makes Armis outwardly powerful and internally brittle:
its order depends on constant enforcement.

### Technology and Advancements

Armis combines metal-element magic, industrial discipline, and state-backed engineering
to produce formidable weapons, armor, and war infrastructure. Its military technology is
not just practical but symbolic. Equipment signals status, authority, and the state's claim
to mastery over both matter and people.

### Homelands and Environment

Armis controls fortified territories across Sky, especially in the northwestern regions.
Its power is concentrated in strongholds, garrisons, and expansion corridors rather than
in loose frontier settlements. The clearest symbol of that power is Armis Citadel, the
fortress-capital from which the empire projects both law and intimidation.

### Political Structure

Armis is ruled by a monarch backed by a high council and an entrenched military order,
but its authority is not as stable as its image suggests. The disappearance of Prince Fox
introduced a lasting fracture into the royal line and opened the door to power struggles
among elite factions that still claim to serve the same ideal of order.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Armis frames conquest as stewardship. Other peoples usually experience it as invasion,
resource extraction, occupation, or cultural erasure. Its expansionist logic puts it in
conflict with almost everyone who values local autonomy, ecological balance, or any form
of social order not sanctioned by Armis itself.

### Additional Details

- Armis helped create the conditions behind the Hex Plague through reckless
  attempts to solve an energy crisis.
- The empire is named for its legendary founder, now remembered in near-mythic terms.
