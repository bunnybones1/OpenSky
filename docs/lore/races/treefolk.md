# Treefolk

![Treefolk](../images/races/Treefolk.png)

The Treefolk are long-lived guardians of the Etherwald whose bodies, memories, and magic
bind them directly to the life of the forest.

### Physical Characteristics

- Bark-bodied beings whose appearance reflects the trees and habitats from which they arise.
- Often marked by visible Ether energy, especially in the eyes.
- Can be small or massive, plain or adorned with moss, flowers, stones, and forest growth.
- Their forms vary by region, making them feel like living expressions of local ecosystems.

### Common Traits

- Patient, observant, and strongly protective of living systems.
- Rich in memory and ecological knowledge.
- Usually quiet or silent in demeanor, but not passive.
- Peaceful by preference and dangerous when their home is threatened.

### Culture and Society

The Treefolk are presented as one of the oldest custodial peoples in Sky, created by
Ethera and the Dryads to maintain balance in the living world. They do not merely inhabit
the forest; they participate in its cycles. When a Treefolk dies, its magic returns to the
soil, reinforcing the sense that the species is less a population than a recurring expression
of the land's memory. They communicate through memory-sharing rather than ordinary
speech, which makes them feel ancient, deliberate, and difficult to deceive.

### Technology and Advancements

The Treefolk do not pursue technology in the industrial sense. Their craft is organic,
magical, and environmental. They shape wood, roots, and surrounding materials into
tools, weapons, or structures when needed, often channeling Ether energy through what
they create.

### Homelands and Environment

They are native above all to the Etherwald, though the source also implies Treefolk can be
found in other heavily earthen or swamp-rich regions. They are adapted to their local
terrain so closely that they can often disappear into it until they choose to act.

### Political Structure

Treefolk society is not portrayed as strongly hierarchical. The eldest serve as guides, and
their deepest loyalties remain with Ethera and the Dryads who shaped their purpose. Their
authority seems to flow from age, memory, and nearness to the forest's will rather than
from formal offices.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Treefolk are not aggressive expansionists and rarely seek conflict beyond their home
territories. But they respond decisively to invasion, especially when forests, roots, or
crystal systems are threatened. They are openly hostile toward Armis incursions
and maintain a protective, almost familial relationship with the Sporekin.

### Magic or Special Abilities

- Strong affinity for earth-aligned magic.
- Deep connection to Ether Crystals as living parts of ecological balance rather than as fuel to be harvested.
- Rare ability to channel crystal energy without uprooting the crystals from the ground.
- Communication through touch, memory transfer, and other forms of telepathic communion.

### Additional Details

- Some Treefolk, called Toh Tem in the source, can house the souls or memories of fallen
  living things until rebirth, turning them into both guardians and places of rest.
