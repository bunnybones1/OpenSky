# Dragons

![Dragons](../images/races/Dragons.png)

The Eight Guardian Dragons of Sky stand above ordinary elemental peoples as ancient powers
responsible for both patronage and balance.

### Role In Sky

- Each dragon is tied to an element and to the peoples shaped most strongly by it.
- Their importance is civilizational as much as personal: they are guardians, symbols, and world-scale forces.
- Stories about Sky's balance often treat the dragons as powers whose conflicts or failures would affect far more than one region.

### Narrative Function

- Dragons are not just monsters or mounts in this setting; they are mythic authorities.
- Individual dragon pages and character bios show that each one expresses its element through a distinct moral and political worldview.
- Taken together, they help frame Sky as a world where elemental power is personal, historical, and sacred.
