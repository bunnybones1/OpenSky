# Sirens

![Sirens](../images/races/Sirens.png)

The Sirens are a carnivorous Merfolk offshoot defined by predation, song, and a social world
where sisterhood and hunger coexist without contradiction.

### Physical Characteristics

- Closely related to Merfolk in overall form, but marked by harsher and more predatory features.
- Often distinguished by black sclera, sharp teeth, and a more overtly dangerous presence.
- Built for speed, underwater stealth, and violent close-range hunting.

### Common Traits

- Strong water affinity paired with specialized predatory behavior.
- Intensely protective of their schools and offspring.
- Capable of beauty that is inseparable from danger.
- Organized enough to hunt cooperatively and defend territory.

### Culture and Society

The Sirens are neither monsters without loyalty nor misunderstood innocents.
They are predators, but they are also kin-bound and socially coherent. Their communal life is
structured around the school, around the protection of their young, and around a sisterhood that
makes collective violence feel intimate rather than chaotic.

### Technology and Advancements

Sirens are tacticians of ambush rather than builders of technology. Their sophistication lies in
hunting method, cooperative movement, territorial use of underwater terrain, and the weaponization
of song, fear, and current.

### Homelands and Environment

They live in dark underwater cave systems and concealed marine lairs that shelter both schools
and young. Those spaces are not only homes but tactical environments. Armis exploitation of mineral-rich caverns has begun to threaten these nurseries directly,
turning ecological damage into a personal and territorial grievance.

### Political Structure

Siren society is matriarchal, with older and more experienced Sirens guiding the school. Their
authority appears grounded in survival knowledge, territorial judgment, and protection of the
young rather than in ceremonial rule for its own sake.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Other peoples fear Sirens because they are legitimate predators, especially toward humans and
Tortugans. Yet their relationships are not purely bestial. They maintain a wary but workable
neutrality with Merfolk and a much sharper hostility toward Armis, whose ecological destruction has
directly endangered Siren young.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Sirens wield entrancing song as both lure and weapon. Combined with water magic, current
control, and coordinated hunting, that gift makes them one of the most terrifying marine threats
in the setting. Their power is not just that they kill, but that they can persuade prey to move
toward death willingly.
