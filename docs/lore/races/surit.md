# Surit

![Surit](../images/races/Surit.png)

The Surit are a sun-revering meerkat people whose desert life is shaped by light magic,
communal village culture, and deep admiration for Rashidan civilization.

### Physical Characteristics

- Small meerkat-like people adapted to desert life.
- Usually associated with sun-baked fur tones and attire shaped by desert and Rashidan influences.
- Their scale and presentation often make them seem modest or unassuming beside larger powers.

### Common Traits

- Strong cultural and magical orientation toward light.
- Generally trusting, teachable, and socially open.
- Capable of craftsmanship and growth when given access to knowledge and mentorship.
- Spiritually and politically drawn toward Rashida.

### Culture and Society

Surit culture is built around reverence for the sun as a source of order, life, and meaning.
That devotion shapes not only worship but also everyday values. They are
earnest rather than grandiose: a people whose spiritual orientation gives coherence to village life
and to their desire to learn from more powerful light-aligned civilizations.

### Technology and Advancements

Surit life is technologically modest in many villages, grounded in practical tools, local craft,
and adaptation to desert conditions. At the same time, the source notes that some Surit leave to
live among the Rashidans, where they learn more advanced craft and magical discipline in exchange
for service and loyalty.

### Homelands and Environment

They are native to the deserts of Rashida, building villages among dunes, stone, and harsh sunlight.
Their environment reinforces both their resilience and their devotion to solar imagery and magic.

### Political Structure

Surit villages appear communal and relatively egalitarian, with consensus and social trust carrying
more weight than rigid hierarchy. Stronger light affinity can elevate
certain individuals into leadership roles.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

The Surit's most important external relationship is with the Rashidans, whom they regard with
religious and cultural admiration. That admiration is not passive. Many Surit actively seek instruction,
craft knowledge, and magical refinement through closer association with Rashida's light-bound traditions.
