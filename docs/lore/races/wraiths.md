# Wraiths

![Wraiths](../images/races/Wraiths.png)

The Wraiths are ageless predators of lifeforce: intelligent, patient, and alien in the way
they understand attachment, death, and the value of a mortal life.

### Physical Characteristics

- Shadow-bound spirits with highly variable, often unsettling humanoid forms.
- Frequently marked by claws, fangs, or impossible anatomical features.
- Built less like a species shaped by biology than like entities shaped by fear, darkness, and appetite.

### Common Traits

- Strong affinity for dark magic and lifeforce manipulation.
- Cunning, observant, and emotionally predatory.
- Young Wraiths are often feral, with intelligence and control emerging over time.
- Capable of cruelty, curiosity, bargaining, or fixation depending on the individual.

### Culture and Society

Wraiths do not read as a conventional society. They are loners, hunters, and watchers more
than citizens. Even so, the source gives them a distinct worldview: life and death are not
moral categories to them so much as energies to be consumed, traded, prolonged, or shaped.
That makes them eerie not only because they kill, but because they often treat mortal
suffering as something intimate, negotiable, and fascinating.

### Technology and Advancements

The Wraiths have no meaningful investment in technology. Their power is elemental and
spiritual, rooted in shadow, memory, fear, and the draining or reshaping of life itself.

### Homelands and Environment

They are associated above all with the Undercroft and with the regions nearest the Void.
The closer one draws to that abyss, the more Wraiths seem to belong there. Some claim they
were born from the Void itself, though that belief remains unresolved. They can remain hidden
in mortal spaces as well, especially in darkness, where they observe or stalk without being seen.

### Political Structure

Wraiths have no formal polity. Their closest equivalent to social structure appears when two
or more of them become interested in the same mortal, memory, or feeding ground. In those
moments they become possessive, territorial, and violently competitive.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Mortals fear Wraiths because fear is rational. They feed on life, bargain with memory, and
can turn grief or desire into leverage. Some prefer direct predation. Others negotiate, offering
to remove pain at a price only they would consider equitable. They may cooperate with Exo or
Revenants when interests align, but they are not fundamentally communal creatures.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Wraiths manipulate shadow, emotion, memory, and lifeforce with intimate precision. They
can hide within darkness, craft personal illusions, and exploit what they learn about a target's
pain or longing. Their power is frightening less because it is spectacular than because it is
psychologically exact.

### Additional Details

- A particularly unsettling Wraith behavior is fixation on a chosen mortal, sometimes for
  that person's entire life.
- That fixation is not framed as love. It is closer to possession, fascination, or long-term
  study, which is part of what makes the Wraiths so disturbing.
