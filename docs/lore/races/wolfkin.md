# Wolfkin

![Wolfkin](../images/races/Wolfkin.png)

The Wolfkin are restless challengers whose culture prizes motion, daring, rivalry, and the
kind of confidence that can look heroic or fatal depending on the day.

### Physical Characteristics

- Wolf- or dog-like people built for speed, resilience, and physical challenge.
- Typically muscular, athletic, and visibly active rather than ceremonial.
- Their appearance can read as intimidating or exuberantly friendly, often at the same time.

### Common Traits

- Competitive, impulsive, and difficult to keep still.
- Drawn toward challenge for its own sake, not merely for reward.
- Socially direct and often more trusting than caution would advise.
- Capable of playfulness and trickery without losing their straightforward edge.

### Culture and Society

Wolfkin culture is built around proving oneself through travel, hardship, and repeated
tests of strength. They are often wanderers, sometimes solitary and sometimes loosely
affiliated, but rarely static. Challenge is not just entertainment for them; it is one of the
main ways they measure worth, build respect, and turn life into story.

### Technology and Advancements

The Wolfkin are not especially defined by technology. Their instincts lean toward physical
solutions, improvisation, and personal prowess, though they are not hostile to tools or
tech when those things prove useful.

### Homelands and Environment

They are associated with mountains, foothills, and other rugged terrain that supports a
life of movement and self-testing. Even when not actively roaming, they prefer landscapes
that can be used for training, competition, and survival.

### Political Structure

The Wolfkin are not presented as a centralized polity. Prestige flows instead toward the
strong, the daring, and the undefeated. Reputation matters more than office, and victory
creates bonds of rivalry as often as it creates hierarchy.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Wolfkin are unusually open to outsiders compared with many proud martial peoples. They
like meeting new rivals, allies, and stories. That openness does not make them foolish, but
it does expose them to risk. Their strongest recurring relationships in the source are a
volatile rivalry with the Raptors and an ongoing game of challenge and trickery with the
Vulpine.

### Additional Details

- Wolfkin are endangered in part because their recklessness gets them killed.
