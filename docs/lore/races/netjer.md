# Net'jer

![Net'jer](../images/races/Netjer.png)

The Net'jer are sacred undead guardians and spirits of Rashida. Bound by devotion rather
than corruption, they continue to protect the living after death and stand as part of the
holy order surrounding Kha Meht and the people of Rashida.

### Physical Characteristics

- Obsidian-blue armor with gold highlights.
- Red jewels embedded in or attached to their armor and bodies.
- Forms that often draw on animals and visual motifs associated with Rashida.

### Common Traits

- Deep loyalty to Kha Meht and to Rashida.
- A restrained, ritualistic demeanor shaped more by duty than emotion.
- A role as watchful protectors rather than independent wanderers.

### Culture and Society

The Net'jer are ancestral guardians. Some are tied to particular families or to the royal
line, but most serve Rashida through Kha Meht, the Dragon of Light. They are
light-aligned, but that does not automatically make them gentle or forgiving. Most are
dutiful protectors; some are aloof, capricious, or dangerous in the rigid way only
immortal spirits can be.

### Geography and Environment they Inhabit

They are found throughout Rashida, especially in tombs, temples, burial grounds, and
other sanctified spaces. Wherever the dead, the divine, and the authority of Kha Meht
overlap, the Net'jer are likely to be present.

### Political Structure

The Net'jer do not appear to govern themselves as a separate polity. They are better
understood as a sacred guardian order embedded within Rashidan religious and civic life.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Their first loyalty is to Kha Meht and to Rashida itself. That devotion can make them
reliable protectors of the living, but it also means they are rarely neutral. They are
guardians first and diplomats second.

### Magic or Special Abilities

The Net'jer wield Light-aligned magic. Their powers include illumination, blessing,
protection, healing, and resistance against dark or hostile spiritual forces, making them
formidable defenders of Rashida's sacred order.
