# Tortugan

![Tortugan](../images/races/Tortugan.png)

The Tortugan are long-lived scholar-engineers whose civilization pairs deep memory,
advanced technology, and a stubborn confidence in methods proven over centuries.

### Physical Characteristics

- Large turtle-like people with heavy shells unique to each individual.
- Often marked by blue-scaled skin, scars, age, and the visible wear of a long life.
- Commonly carry tools, packs, or devices integrated with their shell equipment.
- Their technology often emits a cool blue glow that has become part of their visual identity.

### Common Traits

- Thoughtful, curious, and often eager to explain what they know at great length.
- Highly intellectual, with the source repeatedly emphasizing memory and long historical perspective.
- More open to outsiders than many secluded peoples, even when they come across as dogmatic.
- Prefer peace, but are fully capable of defending themselves and their work.

### Culture and Society

Tortugan culture is shaped by longevity. Their people remember centuries, preserve
promises across generations, and judge ideas against a timescale few others can match.
That gives them unusual wisdom, but it also makes them conservative. They are difficult
to persuade away from established methods because those methods have often carried them
through eras of stability and survival. In their own eyes, they are not merely inventors but
guardians of accumulated world knowledge.

### Technology and Advancements

The Tortugan are among the most technologically sophisticated mortal peoples in Sky.
Their long lifespans allow elders and younger makers to collaborate across centuries of
iteration, producing refined sciences, tools, and systems that few rivals can match. Where
the Lapin are often framed as brilliant mechanics, the Tortugan are framed as civilizational
engineers with unmatched depth of technical continuity.

### Homelands and Environment

They are centered on an island homeland rich in the resources needed for craft, research,
and ecological stewardship. Tortugan development is not
meant to strip the land bare. Their ideal is reciprocal improvement: take from the island,
then strengthen it through knowledge and design.

### Political Structure

Auntuga stands out in the source as a defining leader of Tortugan civilization, associated
with a long era of prosperity and moral authority. More broadly, Tortugan society gives
great weight to elders, not just because of age but because age carries firsthand knowledge
of history, craft, and consequence.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Other peoples can find the Tortugan wise, generous, long-winded, or infuriating depending
on circumstance. Their confidence in their own methods often reads as arrogance. The
setting suggests a stable if opaque understanding with the Masked Dreamers, and a long
tradition of philosophical exchange with the Cygnan.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Tortugan power is usually framed through technology rather than elemental identity, but
some do practice water magic. That talent appears rarer and more restrained than among
peoples whose culture is built primarily around elemental affinity. Auntuga is singled out
as especially gifted in this respect.

### Additional Details

- The Tortugan appear to know routes or methods tied to the Dreamfield and the
  Dream Manor called Arcadia.
- Captain Snapjaw is treated as a notorious Tortugan traitor who traded civic honor for
  piracy and legend.
