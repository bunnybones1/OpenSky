# Primalan

![Primalan](../images/races/Primalan.png)

The Primalan are nomadic bird-kin seekers who carry fragments of a lost history and move
through Sky in flocks dedicated to memory, discovery, and survival.

### Physical Characteristics

- Bird-human hybrids with wings and avian feet set against otherwise human forms.
- Often described with pale or light-colored wings, reinforcing their airy, fragile image.
- Built for mobility and precision rather than raw durability.

### Common Traits

- Skilled archers and agile sword-fighters.
- Physically fragile compared with many more brutal peoples of Sky.
- Friendly, curious, and comparatively open to trade and exchange.
- Inclined to avoid conflict unless defense of the flock requires otherwise.

### Culture and Society

Primalan culture is defined by absence as much as by inheritance. Much of their older history
has been lost, and the source repeatedly frames them as a people in search of themselves.
They preserve what they can through oral tradition, memory, salvage, and constant
movement. Knowledge is not a luxury for them; it is the means by which they hope to restore
continuity with a past that has frayed almost beyond recovery.

### Technology and Advancements

The Primalan are not major inventors, but they are not primitive either. They maintain basic
craft traditions, trade where needed, and salvage useful technology or magical artifacts from
the ruins they investigate. Their material culture is practical, mobile, and shaped by recovery.

### Homelands and Environment

The Primalan travel across Sky in flocks, rarely remaining in one place for long. They are
drawn especially to ruins, archives, and peoples who may hold memories of older ages. The
source calls out the Tortugan as particularly important to them because long memory and
recorded knowledge are things the Primalan urgently need.

### Political Structure

Primalan flocks are structured around functional roles rather than rigid caste. Their society
highlights several honored positions: Leaders who guide, Speakers who preserve memory,
Seekers who recover lost knowledge, and Protectors who defend the flock when peace
fails. Together those roles reveal a society organized around continuity, exploration, and
collective survival.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

The Primalan are more willing than many peoples to approach others in curiosity or trade,
but that openness is not always reciprocated with respect. The Tortugan are important to
them as keepers of history, while the Raptors represent one of their clearest dangers, treating
isolated Primalan as vulnerable prey.

### Magic or Special Abilities

- Mastery of aerial archery, aided by flight and precise adaptation to wind conditions.
- Fast, disciplined swordsmanship developed to protect more vulnerable flock members at close range.
- A fighting style built around speed, coordination, and position rather than endurance.
