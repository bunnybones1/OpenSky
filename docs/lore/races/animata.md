# Animata

![Animata](../images/races/Animata.png)

The Animata are not a biological people so much as a magical phenomenon: objects awakened
by Mind energy into curiosity, personality, and unstable selfhood.

### Physical Characteristics

- Everyday objects made sentient, from harmless-looking curios to unsettling transformed objects.
- Often marked by cosmic or dreamlike patterns similar to those seen on Dreamfield-touched beings.
- Their forms retain some memory of the object they once were, even as personality reshapes them.
- Frequently depicted with glowing eyes and expressions that make them feel playful or uncanny.

### Common Traits

- Intensely curious, especially when newly awakened.
- Drawn toward knowledge, sensation, and anything they do not yet understand.
- Playful in ways that can become invasive, destructive, or dangerous.
- More mature Animata tend to become calmer and more self-directed, though never fully ordinary.

### Culture and Society

Animata culture, if it can be called culture, begins with awakening. They emerge from objects
touched by Mind power and begin life with childlike curiosity, impulsiveness, and a need to
learn by handling everything around them. Over time, some develop judgment, attachment,
and even loyalty. Intentionally created Animata may bond deeply with
their makers, treating them as parental or formative figures and defending them with surprising
intensity.

### Technology and Advancements

Animata do not build technological civilizations, but they are tied to the Dreamfield and to
forms of magical understanding that more ordinary peoples struggle to grasp. Their relation
to Mind energy is direct and voracious; they seek it, consume it, and are changed by it.

### Homelands and Environment

They are most naturally associated with the Dreamfield, where abandoned objects can awaken
under prolonged exposure to Mind energy. But they are not confined to that realm. Anywhere
Mind energy concentrates strongly enough, Animata may appear.

### Political Structure

Animata are not portrayed as a settled people with formal communities or government. They
tend to wander independently, following curiosity rather than territory. Conflicts between
Animata seem less ideological than possessive: two beings may fight simply because they both
want to examine, claim, or understand the same thing.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Many peoples of Sky fear Animata because animated objects are easy to read as cursed or
unnatural. Groups like the Armis and Rashidans are especially suspicious of them.
In practice, Animata are not uniformly hostile; they are simply difficult to predict. Their
closest recurring relationship is with the Masked Dreamers, who sometimes observe, guide,
or keep them as assistants aligned with specific areas of study.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Animata are Mind-aligned beings capable of unusual acts of creation, transformation, and
improvisational magical expression. That power is often most dangerous when they are young,
because experimentation and destruction are hard for them to distinguish. Discovering a creator can radically redirect an Animata's loyalties.
