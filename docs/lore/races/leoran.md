# Leoran

![Leoran](../images/races/Leorans.png)

A proud Catlike people with a natural affinity for lightning magic and a knack for adopting new allies.

### Physical Characteristics

- Catlike people with multiple visible lineages, including lionlike, tigerlike, and sphinxlike forms.
- Frequently associated with elegant weapons whose design signals wealth, honor, or personal identity.
- Built for speed, poise, and striking presence rather than brute mass alone.

### Common Traits

- Strong natural affinity for lightning and electrically charged weaponry.
- Mobile, alert, and culturally comfortable with travel.
- Proud, moody, and not always easy to read at first meeting.
- Quick to form deep bonds once trust is earned.

### Culture and Society

The Leoran are presented as a proud people whose identity blends martial grace, elemental
talent, and movement across Sky. Though their roots lie in desert lands, many now live
nomadically, traveling rather than binding themselves to fixed cities. Their culture seems to
value independence and pride without abandoning kinship; belonging can be chosen as well as
inherited.

### Technology and Advancements

The Leoran do not prioritize technology as a civilizational ideal. Their strengths lie instead
in lightning magic, martial discipline, and portable forms of skill that travel well with the
people themselves.

### Homelands and Environment

Leoran communities are scattered across Sky. Some older population centers remain in desert
regions, but many Leoran prefer movement over permanence. That wandering life broadens
their contact with other peoples and helps explain their unusual talent for absorbing outsiders
into chosen family structures.

### Political Structure

The Leoran are not framed as a centralized state. Authority appears local to clans, bands,
or traveling groups, each with its own leaders and customs. Independence matters, but so
does a strong wider sense of Leoran kinship.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

The Leoran can seem wary or volatile at first, but the source emphasizes how quickly they
may turn affection into fierce solidarity once someone proves trustworthy. Their practice of
"adopting" outsiders into family is one of their clearest defining traits and gives them a
distinct social warmth beneath the pride and guardedness.

### Magic or Special Abilities

The Leoran wield lightning with both elegance and force. Their elemental affinity supports
direct spell use as well as weapons designed to channel and amplify electrical power, making
them dangerous at close range and at a distance.
