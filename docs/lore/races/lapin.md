# Lapin

![Lapin](../images/races/Lapin.png)

The Lapin are rabbit-like engineers, miners, and makers whose technical brilliance is matched
by industrious pride, clan warmth, and a permanent competitive streak.

### Physical Characteristics

- Small rabbit-like people, usually compact in build and visibly adapted to busy practical work.
- Broad visual variation in ears, markings, and species traits is implied by the source.
- Frequently associated with gear, mech interfaces, and workshop equipment that amplify their presence.

### Common Traits

- Intensely inventive and rarely content to stop refining an idea.
- Sensitive about being underestimated, especially because of size.
- Proud of craft, community, and visible technical achievement.
- Locked in recurring rivalry with the Badgerkin.

### Culture and Society

Lapin culture revolves around making. They are builders, mechanics, miners, and tinkerers
whose work has tangible impact across Sky. They are good-natured, but not passive. Their pride in invention is serious, and social standing appears closely tied to
what one can design, construct, improve, or profitably deploy.

### Technology and Advancements

The Lapin are one of the clearest technological peoples in the setting. They create complex
machines, mechs, tools, and industrial systems that shape everyday life far beyond their own
communities. That same drive also creates friction: mining, expansion, and construction can
disrupt neighboring peoples and landscapes when Lapin ambition outpaces restraint.

### Homelands and Environment

Lapin settlements are described less as polished capitals than as dense working habitats:
tunnels, stacked structures, improvised workshop districts, and industrial mazes built around
resource extraction and active invention. Their towns feel lived-in by people who are always
mid-project.

### Political Structure

Lapin society appears meritocratic in craft rather than rigidly aristocratic. Inventiveness,
technical success, and practical usefulness carry real weight. At the same time, the source
emphasizes familial social language and community loyalty, suggesting a culture that mixes
competitive achievement with strong kin-like bonds.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

The Lapin are protective of their designs and wary of anyone who might exploit them without
proper respect or payment. Their rivalry with the Badgerkin is one of the clearest cultural
relationships in the source: close enough for comparison, different enough for endless friction.
They also look up to Tortugan sophistication and will work with Armis when money, machinery,
or engineering contracts outweigh political scruple.

### Magic or Special Abilities

The Lapin are not primarily defined by innate magic. Their extraordinary power comes from
engineering, mechanical design, mining expertise, and the ability to turn cleverness into
machines that alter the balance of power around them.
