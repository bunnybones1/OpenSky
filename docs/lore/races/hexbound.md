# Hexbound

![Hexbound](../images/races/Hexbound.png)

The Hexbound are plague-ridden undead driven by corrupted crystal growth rather than by
ordinary will. To most of Sky they appear as a mindless horde, but their spread suggests a
larger logic moving through every infected body.

### Common Traits

- Created through the Crystal Plague, a corruption tied to damaged Ether Crystal systems.
- Distinct from Revenants and Wraiths: the Hexbound are not a functioning undead society.
- Driven to spread, infect, and repurpose bodies, creatures, and sometimes even objects.

### Nature Of The Threat

The Hexbound behave like extensions of a larger crystal intelligence. Individual infected
bodies may appear mindless, but the Hex itself does not. It spreads with coherence,
repurposes living systems for expansion, and builds toward ends that the peoples of Sky do
not yet understand.

### Origin and Meaning

The Crystal Plague is closely tied to scientific overreach. Attempts to solve an energy crisis
through self-replenishing crystal technology instead produced a corruption that infected both
crystals and the living. What began as a technical solution became one of the setting's most
terrifying systemic disasters.

### Relationship To Other Threats

One of the strangest aspects of the Hex is its apparent resistance to the Glitch. Where the
Glitch distorts and repurposes nearly everything it touches, the Hex remains coherent. That
makes it more than a plague. It is a rival corruption with its own logic, momentum, and
possible end state.
