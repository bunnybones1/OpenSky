# Masked Dreamers

![Masked Dreamers](../images/races/MaskedDreamers.png)

The Masked Dreamers are an occult order of dream-walkers, archivists, and keepers of forbidden
balance whose survival depends on maintaining Yxxathoxzz's sleep.

### Physical Characteristics

- Humanoids whose public identity is inseparable from ritual masks, veils, and dream-marked regalia.
- Their masks appear to function as symbols of office, protection, and psychological distance.
- The order's visual language suggests secrecy, ceremony, and deliberate separation from ordinary civic life.

### Common Traits

- Secretive, disciplined, and far more patient than the panic they often inspire.
- Drawn to dreams, memory, and knowledge that other peoples would classify as dangerous.
- Burdened by a custodial mindset: they do not merely study terrible things, they manage them.

### Culture and Society

The Masked Dreamers are defined by proximity to unacceptable knowledge. They operate near
the Dreamfield and around the sleeping horror of Yxxathoxzz, feeding it dreams and memory to
keep catastrophe deferred. That task shapes everything about them. Masks help turn the self
into an office: the individual matters less than the role, the vow, and the continuity of the work.
The order therefore reads less like a normal faction and more like a priesthood built around a
problem no one else is willing or able to hold.

### Technology and Advancements

Their craft is cognitive, ritual, and occult rather than industrial. They specialize in dream-
management, memory handling, sleep rites, and the guarded circulation of knowledge too
volatile to move freely through Sky.

### Homelands and Environment

They are tied to the margins of the Dreamfield, to hidden sanctums, and to places where the
boundary between waking thought and dream-state can be monitored or manipulated. Their
strongholds are likely chosen for secrecy and metaphysical usefulness rather than for ordinary
strategic visibility.

### Political Structure

The order appears hierarchical, initiatory, and role-bound. Knowledge itself functions as rank:
those trusted with deeper truths also bear greater danger and heavier responsibility. Leadership
within the Masked Dreamers likely depends as much on what one can safely contain as on what
one can command.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Most outsiders have reason to fear or resent them. The Masked Dreamers traffic in secrets,
operate behind ritual concealment, and maintain a relationship with Yxxathoxzz that would look
like blasphemy or treason if fully exposed. Yet they are not simple cultists. They are custodians
of one of the setting's ugliest necessities, which makes them both morally compromised and
potentially indispensable.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Their power is tied to dream navigation, memory exchange, sleep rites, concealment of identity,
and negotiated access to the knowledge trapped within Yxxathoxzz's vast interior archive.
