# Badgerkin

![Badgerkin](../images/races/Badgerkin.png)

The Badgerkin are small, fearless tinkerers with a love of explosives, tunneling, and fun
that borders on civic hazard. They combine technical skill with gleeful recklessness,
making them equal parts inventor, saboteur, and folk hero.

### Physical Characteristics

- Anthropomorphic badger-like people, typically around 3-4 feet tall.
- Heavy-duty tools, goggles, gear, and mining equipment are common.
- Often depicted with long white hair and sturdy, practical builds.

### Common Traits

- Playful, chaotic, and deeply resistant to overbearing authority.
- Love loud solutions, dramatic engineering, and impractical fun.
- Proud of their technical skill and delighted by anything that explodes on schedule.

### Culture and Society

Badgerkin culture is communal, boisterous, and invention-driven. They trade ideas,
compete with one another, and often cooperate on projects that are equal parts ingenious
and irresponsible. Noise complaints are likely a routine feature of Badgerkin life.

### Technology and Advancements

They are elite makers of munitions, blasting tools, and breaching equipment. Where the
Lapin represent refinement and disciplined engineering, the Badgerkin represent
improvisation, audacity, and explosive problem-solving.

### Geography and Environment they Inhabit

Badgerkin are natural burrowers and tunnelers. They thrive underground, in mines,
workshops, and improvised warrens where both engineering and demolition can happen
with minimal supervision.

### Political Structure

Their society appears loosely organized and highly suspicious of rigid hierarchy. Shared
customs, practical trust, and mutual benefit matter more than formal governance.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Badgerkin are sociable, mischievous, and often eager to rope outsiders into whatever they
are doing. Their best-known relationship in the source is a rivalry with the Lapin,
combining mutual respect, pranks, and competing engineering philosophies.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Badgerkin are not defined by innate magic. Their strength lies in technical ingenuity,
improvised devices, and the ability to weaponize a workshop.
