# Cygnan

![Cygnan](../images/races/Cygnan.png)

The Cygnan are secluded monastic scholars-warriors who pursue enlightenment through
discipline, memory, and relentless physical training.

### Physical Characteristics

- Swanlike people with beaks, avian grace, and a distinctly austere bearing.
- Notably wingless, a trait the source frames as spiritually meaningful rather than lacking.
- Usually dressed in monastic garments suited to training, study, and mountain life.

### Common Traits

- Highly disciplined in both body and mind.
- Guarded with outsiders and selective about who earns trust.
- Custodians of old histories, techniques, and restricted knowledge.
- Often associated with elite unarmed combat and refined air magic.

### Culture and Society

Cygnan culture is built on the idea that understanding is earned through discipline, not
granted by comfort. Training begins early and appears to continue throughout life. Their
monasteries are not simply places of worship but institutions of memory, physical rigor,
and moral selection. They are capable of welcoming outsiders, but only rarely and only
when those outsiders prove themselves worthy of instruction.

### Technology and Advancements

The Cygnan do not define themselves through technological expansion. They maintain the
crafts needed to sustain monastic life, preserve records, and equip their warriors, but
their highest achievements lie in knowledge, training systems, and spiritual technique
rather than machinery.

### Homelands and Environment

They live in mountain monasteries far from the main political centers of Sky. Those sites
serve as both sanctuaries and archives, storing scrolls and teachings that preserve long
views of the world's history.

### Political Structure

Cygnan society is guided by Elders chosen for mastery rather than inheritance. Authority
flows from discipline, wisdom, and the ability to embody the teachings. That does not
make their culture gentle. Their standards are severe, and those who cannot keep pace
may be quietly marginalized even without formal condemnation.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

The Cygnan are not naturally expansionist or warlike, but their secrecy and aloofness can
make them seem arrogant or withholding to outsiders. They appear to have a tradition of
intellectual exchange with the Tortugan, while their relationship with the Raptors is more
openly adversarial in temperament, even when it stops short of outright war.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Advanced Cygnan practitioners are associated with air magic expressed through speed,
grace, and precision. At the highest levels, their movement is described almost as if they
become part of the wind itself.

### Additional Details

- Lotus and Iris are among the few named outsiders tied closely to Cygnan training.
- Titus is noted as someone the Cygnan accepted for a time, but whose compassion pulled
  him away from their path.
- The Cygnan are also tied to Alcazar and to vows of silence around dangerous
  historical knowledge.
