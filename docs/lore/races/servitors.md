# Servitors

![Servitors](../images/races/Servitors.png)

The Servitors are ancient living machines from the era of the Qui, remembered less as a people
than as a surviving puzzle moving through the world with motives no one fully understands.

### Physical Characteristics

- Ancient mechanical entities built from articulated metal and construct-like forms.
- Not consistently humanoid, which reinforces their alien and premodern origin.
- Expressionless by design, forcing others to interpret intent through action alone.

### Common Traits

- Distant, enigmatic, and emotionally unreadable.
- Rarely seen in large numbers or in sustained cooperation.
- Nomadic in practice, even if their movements may serve hidden patterns.
- Capable of feats that look like lost technology, unknown magic, or both.

### Culture and Society

The Servitors are framed as near-extinct survivors of an older order, carrying forward designs,
languages, and purposes that outlasted the civilization that made them. They occasionally
intervene in mortal affairs, but never in ways that make their deeper intentions obvious. A
Servitor may save someone, ignore them, or abandon them with equal calm. That ambiguity is
central to how the source treats them.

### Technology and Advancements

Servitor design is beyond the understanding of other known peoples, including the most advanced
mortals. Their bodies and functions imply a level of craft no one in the current age can reliably
replicate, which makes every Servitor both an entity and an archaeological problem.

### Homelands and Environment

They are encountered across Sky, but most often in isolated, desolate, or forgotten places:
ruins, margins, dead zones, and landscapes where history feels half-buried. Their lack of
settlements only deepens the sense that they are following instructions no one else can see.

### Political Structure

No functioning Servitor polity is visible in the current age. They seem to act alone, but not
randomly. What looks like solitude may actually be obedience
to ancient directives, distributed routines, or command structures that mortals can no longer detect.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Other peoples can observe the Servitors, but rarely understand them. Their interactions with
mortals produce speculation rather than knowledge. One of the most suggestive details in the
source is that two Servitors speaking to one another use a language no one has deciphered.
That language remains one of the strongest clues that their true history is still intact somewhere
inside them.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Servitors can heal, intervene, and perform acts that appear miraculous or impossibly advanced.
Because no one can separate their "technology" from their "magic," the distinction stops being
useful. Their capabilities are best understood as remnants of a lost system of power.
