# Raptors

![Raptors](../images/races/Raptors.png)

The Raptors are predatory bird-people of Sky's outer regions, feared for their vigilance,
violence, and the constant tension between instinct and emerging sophistication.

### Physical Characteristics

- Fully avian humanoids, distinct from the more human-shaped Primalan.
- Broad variation across predatory bird forms, all emphasizing flight, talons, and killing power.
- Physically built for hunting, aerial dominance, and intimidation.

### Common Traits

- Highly vigilant and intensely territorial.
- Strong hunters even when not formally organized for war.
- Some show meaningful affinity for air magic.
- Often judge others first through threat, strength, and bearing.

### Culture and Society

Raptor society is diverse, but the source repeatedly frames it through predation, territorial
instinct, and subgroup variation. Some flocks operate almost entirely by dominance and
impulse. Others are more disciplined, strategic, and negotiable without ceasing to be
dangerous. That range matters: the Raptors are not uniformly bestial, but even their more
sophisticated forms remain shaped by the logic of hunter cultures.

### Technology and Advancements

Raptor technological development is uneven. Many groups rely on basic tools and natural
fortification, while more sophisticated flocks can produce more complex structures and
command hierarchies. Those higher-order groups are also the ones most likely to entertain
negotiation instead of defaulting immediately to violence.

### Homelands and Environment

Raptors are highly adaptable and can inhabit forests, cliffs, grasslands, and other open or
elevated spaces suited to flight and hunting. Different subgroups choose environments that
match their strengths, but the broader pattern is mobility tied to predatory advantage.

### Political Structure

Political structure varies by flock. Some are ruled simply by dominance. Others develop more
layered hierarchies based on strength, strategy, or command ability. They also emphasize
a dueling culture between subgroups, where testing one another's strength is a way of deciding
respect before trust, cooperation, or restraint.

### Relationships with Other Races/Factions

Raptors can coexist with some peoples at a distance, but several relationships in the source
are explicitly conflict-heavy. They clash with Armis over territory, regard the Cygnan with
mutual disdain, intensify the Wolfkin's reckless mortality through competition, and prey on
isolated Primalan. These patterns make the Raptors one of the clearest recurring dangers in
the faction network.

### Magic or Special Abilities

Raptors excel at flight, aerial maneuver, and natural-weapon combat. Some also wield air
magic. Even alone they are dangerous hunters; in coordinated groups they become much more
frightening, turning speed and altitude into collective force.
