# Races and Factions

Peoples, factions, traditions, and world-scale forces across Sky.

## Peoples and Civilizations

- [Animata](./animata.md): Sentient objects shaped by Mind energy, curiosity, and gradual self-discovery.
- [Armis](./armis.md): A militarized human empire built on hierarchy, discipline, and the belief that order must be imposed by force.
- [Badgerkin](./badgerkin.md): Small, fearless tinkerers whose love of explosives, tunneling, and fun borders on civic hazard.
- [Cygnan](./cygnan.md): Secluded scholar-warriors who pursue enlightenment through discipline, memory, and relentless training.
- [Dryads](./dryads.md): Ethera's daughters and some of the oldest protectors of the Etherwald.
- [Exo](./exo.md): Feared insect-people of the Undercroft, shaped by exile, paranoia, and survival.
- [Hoplites](./hoplites.md): Disciplined shield-warriors who tie civic order, warfare, and sacrifice into one public ethic.
- [Lapin](./lapin.md): Rabbit-like engineers and miners whose technical brilliance is matched by clan pride and rivalry.
- [Leoran](./leoran.md): Proud catlike lightning-kin with a talent for adoption, alliance, and sudden force.
- [Merfolk](./merfolk.md): Ancient ocean guardians who treat the sea as both homeland and sacred trust.
- [Net’jer](./netjer.md): Sacred undead guardians and spirits bound to Rashida's religious order.
- [Oni](./oni.md): Mountain warrior-clans defined by strength, hierarchy, craft, and a severe code of honor.
- [Penguindale](./penguindale.md): A hardy polar people defined by communal survival, ritual life, and cold-weather craft.
- [Polar Bears](./polar-bears.md): Massive northern fisher-hunters whose culture prizes endurance, territory, and provision.
- [Primalan](./primalan.md): Nomadic bird-kin seekers who carry fragments of a lost history across Sky.
- [Raptors](./raptors.md): Predatory bird-people feared for vigilance, violence, and the tension between instinct and sophistication.
- [Rashidans](./rashidans.md): The sacred desert civilization of Rashida, where kingship, burial, and solar order remain politically alive.
- [Revenants](./revenants.md): A highly organized undead civilization of the Undercroft.
- [Royal Mummies](./royal-mummies.md): Revered undead rulers whose duty to Rashida continues after death.
- [Saurian](./saurian.md): Reptilian frontier peoples defined by endurance, territorial memory, and survival logic.
- [Servitors](./servitors.md): Ancient living machines from the era of the Qui, remembered as a surviving puzzle more than a people.
- [Sirens](./sirens.md): A predatory Merfolk offshoot whose hunger and sisterhood are inseparable.
- [Sporekin](./sporekin.md): Gentle mushroom-folk whose communal life is shaped by kinship, caution, and the value of their spores.
- [Surit](./surit.md): Sun-revering meerkat people whose desert life centers on light magic and communal devotion.
- [Tortugan](./tortugan.md): Long-lived scholar-engineers who pair deep memory with advanced craft.
- [Treefolk](./treefolk.md): Forest guardians whose bodies, memories, and magic are bound to living ecosystems.
- [Valkyrn](./valkyrn.md): The broken remnants of a once-great people still marked by Cryogen's fall.
- [Vulpine](./vulpine.md): Fox-like tricksters, duelists, and illusionists with a surprisingly strict sense of honor.
- [Wolfkin](./wolfkin.md): Restless challengers whose culture prizes motion, daring, and rivalry.
- [Wraiths](./wraiths.md): Ageless predators of lifeforce who understand attachment and death in alien terms.

## Orders, Factions, and Special Groups

- [Masked Dreamers](./masked-dreamers.md): Dream custodians who keep Yxxathoxzz asleep through ritualized exchange of memory and dreams.
- [Ifrit](./ifrit.md): An ascetic fire tradition tied to Oni mystics and mountain orders.
- [Wu-Kin](./wu-kin.md): Guardians of ancient ruins who protect dangerous buried knowledge.

## World-Scale Forces and Conditions

- [Dreambeasts](./dreambeasts.md): Dreamfield entities born from thought, fear, and emotion rather than stable biology.
- [Hexbound](./hexbound.md): Plague-ridden undead animated by corrupted crystal growth and a larger hidden logic.
- [Dragons](./dragons.md): Ancient elemental guardians whose power shapes peoples, regions, and the balance of Sky.
- [Elementals](./elementals.md): Living manifestations of elemental conditions, shaped by the health or corruption of their surroundings.
