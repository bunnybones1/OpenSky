# Kha Meth, Elemental Dragon of Light

![Kha Meth, Elemental Dragon of Light](../images/characters/khamet.png)

### Firstborn of Sky, the Eternal Sun of Rashida

- Age: Eldest of the Dragons
- Height: 120' long
- Personality: Proud, uncompromising, majestic, and fiercely protective.
- Motivation: To protect Sky and eradicate darkness wherever he believes it threatens the world.
- Relationship: Patron deity of Rashida and de facto first among the elemental dragons.

## Backstory

Kha Meth is framed as the eldest and most sovereign of the dragons: a being of blazing
authority whose presence is inseparable from Rashida's identity. He is worshipped as a
god, feared as a force of judgment, and treated by many as one of the oldest living powers
in all of Sky.

His protection is not gentle. Kha Meth embodies light as revelation, purification, and
unquestioned radiance. That makes him a guardian to his followers, but also a dangerous
absolutist. He does not merely oppose darkness; he treats it as contamination, which puts
him in philosophical and moral conflict with anyone who sees balance rather than
extermination as the answer.

## Additional Details

- Worshipped by both Rashidans and, in some traditions, the Surit.
- Said by some to destroy not only bodies but souls.
- His hostility toward Darkness puts him at odds with figures such as Bouran.
- Beloved, feared, and politically central in any story that touches Rashida.
