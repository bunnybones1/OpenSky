# Sitti, the original (deceased)

![Sitti, the original (deceased)](../images/characters/sitti-original.png)

- Prism: Heart + Intellect
- Quote: "Together, we can make Sky a better place for everyone."
- Age: 31 | Height: 1.80cm | Weight: 70kg
- Personality: Altruistic, caring, devoted to helping others.
- Motivation: To help all those she could, heal the sick, ease the world's pain, and make Sky a better place.
- Relationship: Was well liked by those OpenSkys who knew her, but around 6-7 years ago, unknown to them, she passed away, and her sister took her place.

### Additional Details

- Twin sister of 'Sitti' the current HRT/INT OpenSky.
- Elder of the two by 10 minutes.
- Respected 'The Cycle' the same as Bouran does,
- Believed that greater knowledge of medicine and science could better the lives of all Sky's people.
- Was quite bright herself but believed her sister (the current sitti) was even more brilliant, and that between her sister's genius, and her abilites as a OpenSky, they would be able to improve the lives of everyone in Sky.
- Came down with a mysterious illness that ate away at her.
- Her sister tried everything to help her, but could not save her.
- When she died, her sister (current Sitti) took up her name and identity, and bound the HRT/INT prism to herself via dark magic, vowing to see her sister's dreams completed.
