# Dracomantium, Elemental Dragon of Metal

![Dracomantium, Elemental Dragon of Metal](../images/characters/dracomantium.png)

### Bulwark of the meek, tender yet unbreakable, youngest of her kin

- Quote: "I will shield the small from harm."
- Age: Youngest of the Dragons
- Personality: Kind, selfless, protective, and occasionally clumsy.
- Motivation: To protect mortals and to prove that strength is measured by what it shelters.
- Relationship: Beloved by many mortals, though some elder dragons regard her as inexperienced.

## Backstory

Dracomantium is the youngest of the elemental dragons and the one most openly drawn to
mortals. Where some of her kin rule from distance, terror, or abstraction, she intervenes
personally. She shields the vulnerable, takes interest in invention and craftsmanship, and
sees dignity in the struggles of ordinary people.

That closeness to mortals also seems to define her central tragedy. She once took mortal form and founded Armis in the hope
of creating a civilization that would stand as a shield for the world. Instead, that ideal
hardened into authoritarianism. She abandoned it, but the failure still hangs over her as a
private wound and a source of shame.

## Additional Details

- Born without wings and later fashioned them for herself.
- Can bless mortals by making parts of their bodies crystalline, stronger, and more
  durable.
- Frequently uses her own body as a living shield.
- Sees justice and protection as the highest expression of strength.
