# The Arcadeum Mask

![The Arcadeum Mask](../images/characters/arcadeum.png)

The Arcadeum Mask is the mask itself, not the person wearing it. It houses the spirit of an imprisoned hoarder of knowledge who can only act in the world through a living host.

## Core Premise

- The trapped intellect inside the mask once sought to monopolize Sky's knowledge.
- Meng'Long is said to have imprisoned that spirit within the mask.
- The mask can only operate by binding itself to a wearer.
- The current host is believed to be connected to Ari's family.

## Narrative Use

The mask works as a parasitic source of insight, temptation, and compromised agency. Any story involving it should treat knowledge as both advantage and contamination.
