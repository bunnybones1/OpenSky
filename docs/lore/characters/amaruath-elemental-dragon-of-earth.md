# Amaruath, Elemental Dragon of Earth

![Amaruath, Elemental Dragon of Earth](../images/characters/amaruath.png)

### Eldest Son of Ethera, King of the Etherwald, Great-Father

- Quote: "All life in Sky is under my protection"
- Personality: Level headed, calm, kind, and slow to anger, but fiercely protective of those he watches over.
- Motivation: Protector of the Etherwald and all forests within Sky.
- Relationship: Mate to Tiamat, gets along well with most other Dragons, serves as a voice of reason among them, being more level headed than most.

## Additional Details

- Some say he controls seasons - winter happens when he sleeps.
- Few have ever seen him, as he resides in The Etherwald.
- On rare occasions, people have seen him flying overhead.
- Wherever Amaruath passes, trees and plants sprout.
