# Iris, the Loreseeker

![Iris, the Loreseeker](../images/characters/iris.png)

- Prism: Wisdom + Agility
- Age: 30 | Height: 1.7m | Weight: 54kg
- Personality: Spirited, devout, and purposeful.
- Motivation: The pursuit of knowledge and self-understanding.
- Relationship: Associated with Zoey and other travelers encountered on her journeys.

### Backstory

Iris is a scholar-pilgrim whose pursuit of knowledge is inseparable from her search for
meaning. Raised among the Cygnans, she devoted herself early to study, language, and
the deeper history of Sky. That discipline eventually grew into a wandering life: Iris set
out across Sky to learn directly from the lands, peoples, ruins, and forgotten traditions
that formal study alone could not reach.

Her path is part pilgrimage, part investigation. She seeks old tombs, ancient scripts, and
lost knowledge not simply to catalogue them, but to understand what Sky is and what it
asks of the people who inhabit it. That search may eventually become more intimate and protective through an adopted child whose nature is unusual and significant.

### Additional Details

- Apprentice of the Cygnans from a young age.
- After receiving her Prism, she sought out Lotus for deeper understanding.
- In her youth, she traveled across Sky gathering knowledge of its lands and peoples.
- She studies ancient tombs, languages, and older layers of Sky's history.
- She is positioned as a master of languages and a seeker of the deeper "Sky Language."
