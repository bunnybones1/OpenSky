# The Training Bot

![The Training Bot](../images/characters/trainingbot.png)

The Training Bot is a practical construct built to teach, test, and normalize controlled combat.
It belongs to the part of OpenSky's world that turns dangerous magic into something trainable.

## Role in the Setting

The bot is less important as an individual personality than as a sign of institutional maturity.
A culture capable of building dedicated training constructs has moved beyond improvising martial
education one duel at a time. The Training Bot therefore points toward academies, formalized
practice, and engineered repetition.

## Narrative Use

- It can serve as a neutral tutorial figure without needing heroic stature.
- It reinforces the idea that OpenSky combat is taught, studied, and systematized.
- It provides a believable bridge between game tutorial framing and in-world logic.

## Tone

The Training Bot should read as functional rather than mythic: reliable, limited, and memorable
because of what it reveals about the world that built it.
