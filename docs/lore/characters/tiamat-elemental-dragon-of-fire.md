# Tiamat, Elemental Dragon of Fire

![Tiamat, Elemental Dragon of Fire](../images/characters/tiamat.png)

### The Queen of Flames, the forge from which heroes are born

- Quote: "The passion of fire burns within us all."
- Age: Third eldest of the Dragons
- Personality: Passionate, volatile, proud, and fiercely caring.
- Motivation: To shape strength and conviction in others, and to destroy cowardice or injustice.
- Relationship: Reveres those who pursue ideals with courage and treats many fire-aligned beings as her children.

## Backstory

Tiamat embodies the double nature of fire: creation and destruction, inspiration and ruin.
She is framed not merely as a force of burning violence, but as a dragon of trial and
forging. In her presence, weakness is exposed, but so is potential.

She is maternal and terrifying at once. She watches over her
children, whether biological or chosen, but does so with the uncompromising standards of
a being who believes strength must be tempered in flame. She values conviction, justice,
and courage, and reserves special contempt for cowardice or weak will.

## Additional Details

- Dwells within the heart of the largest volcano in Sky.
- Her three heads have distinct personalities, but share one core purpose.
- Associated with champions, ideals, and the hardening of heroic resolve.
- Mother to many smaller dragons and fire elementals.
