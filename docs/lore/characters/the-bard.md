# The Bard

The Bard is one of the chaos-aligned figures tied to Yxxathoxzz: not a straightforward minstrel,
but a herald of disorder who turns performance into corruption, seduction, and psychic pressure.

## Core Idea

The Bard represents one of the more intimate faces of chaos in Sky. Where Yxxathoxzz is vast and
inhuman, the Bard is legible, theatrical, and mobile. He carries catastrophe in a form that can
still converse, charm, and recruit.

## Role in the Setting

- He is aligned with Yxxathoxzz rather than with any conventional state or faction.
- He likely functions as an intermediary between cosmic hunger and mortal temptation.
- His presence suggests that chaos in Sky is not only destructive, but expressive and contagious.

## Narrative Use

The Bard works best as a destabilizer. He should feel like someone who can enter a room, a camp,
or a court and leave its moral boundaries weaker than he found them. His danger lies in making
corruption sound meaningful, beautiful, or inevitable.
