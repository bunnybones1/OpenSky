# Gusto, Elemental Dragon of Air

![Gusto, Elemental Dragon of Air](../images/characters/gusto.png)

### Horizon Lord, unstoppable storm, eternal traveler

- Quote: "He is freedom."
- Age: Second eldest of the Dragons
- Height: 80' long
- Personality: Aloof, enigmatic, and fiercely independent.
- Motivation: To preserve freedom, especially against tyrants and those who would cage others.
- Relationship: Keeps his distance from both dragons and mortals, though he is remembered and revered by some wanderers.

## Backstory

Gusto is the dragon of freedom in its purest and most dangerous form. He spends his time
near the outer reaches of Sky, far from the structures and obligations that bind most other
beings. He rarely intervenes, but when he does, it is usually in opposition to domination,
control, or coercion.

He is both elusive and overwhelming. He prefers distance over rule,
but remains one of the most fearsome beings in the setting, capable of calling hurricanes,
typhoons, and mist around himself at will. Even his speech is rare and consequential,
which reinforces the sense that Gusto acts only when something truly matters.

## Additional Details

- Can dissolve into mist and is nearly impossible to catch.
- Speaks rarely, often in single words.
- Each word he chooses to speak is treated in the source as unusually powerful.
- Strongly associated with the horizon, travel, weather, and ungoverned space.
