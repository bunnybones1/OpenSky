# Yxxathoxzz, the Oblivion

Yxxathoxzz is the sleeping devourer at the center of the Dreamfield: a setting-scale entity that
consumes dreams, memory, knowledge, and eventually anything else that can be known.

## Core Nature

Yxxathoxzz is best understood as both predator and archive. What it devours is not merely
obliterated. It is retained, absorbed, and made part of a vast interior hoard of thought and
history. That makes it more horrifying than a simple destroyer. It feeds by accumulation.

## Relationship to the Dreamfield

The Dreamfield may be shaped by Yxxathoxzz as much as it contains it. The creature's presence
helps explain why the realm treats distance, time, and identity as unstable. In narrative terms,
it is the gravitational center of dream-related danger across the setting.

## The Masked Dreamers

The Masked Dreamers maintain a precarious arrangement with Yxxathoxzz by feeding it dreams
and knowledge to keep it asleep. In return, they gain access to fragments of what it has already
consumed. That exchange is useful, but it is also morally compromised and catastrophically
fragile. If the flow stops, Yxxathoxzz does not merely wake. It wakes hungry.

## Role in the Setting

Yxxathoxzz is not a conventional character arc waiting to happen. It is an existential condition
hanging over Sky: a reminder that the world contains appetites too large for ordinary heroism to
solve cleanly.
