# Mira, the Mechanist

![Mira, the Mechanist](../images/characters/mira.png)

- Prism: Strength + Intellect
- Age: 18 | Height: 1.50m | Weight: 42kg
- Personality: Brilliant, inventive, anxious, and intensely competitive.
- Motivation: To build inventions that can protect others and to prove herself through her work.
- Relationship: Lifelong friend and rival of Mai.

### Backstory

Mira's childhood was defined by catastrophe and reconstruction. After a devastating
accident destroyed her family, she ended up in an orphanage, where she found refuge in
books about mechanics and engineering. By the age of nine, she had already built her
first mecha, an early sign that invention would become both her craft and her armor.

Mira and Mai were eventually carried out of a war-torn Armis by Banjo, who smuggled
them through the Dreamfield to safety. He entrusted them to Althea in Kore City, where
the two girls were raised together and became inseparable. Their bond was affectionate,
but never passive: each pushed the other to become more capable, more daring, and more
ambitious.

Under Althea's care, Mira learned not only manufacturing and repair, but also the idea
that invention should serve people rather than dominate them. That ideal hardened after
Althea's disappearance. Since then, Mira's work has become both personal and
protective. She wants to build machines strong enough to shield the innocent from the
kind of loss that shaped her own life.

### Additional Details

- In her youth, Mira lived close to a scrapyard or another place where mechanical junk
  could be found, and she used to tinker and invent things alongside Mai.
- Mira and Mai became OpenSkys at the same time, with paired prism combinations.
- Mira's style of invention is methodical: planning, simulation, testing, and careful
  iteration.
- She is physically the smallest OpenSky, but in her `KRS-1555` Kinetic Robotic
  Superarmor she towers over the rest.
- Her inventions tend to focus on strength, durability, and protection.
