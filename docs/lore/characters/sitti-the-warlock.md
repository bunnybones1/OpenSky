# Sitti, the Warlock

![Sitti, the Warlock](../images/characters/sitti.png)

- Prism: Heart + Intellect
- Quote: "True knowledge requires sacrifices."
- Age: 31 | Height: 1.80m | Weight: 70kg
- Personality: Charismatic, disciplined, controlling, and morally unrestrained.
- Motivation: To conquer death by seizing control of the cycle itself.
- Relationship: Treats most people as instruments, obstacles, or raw material in service of her larger goal.

## Backstory

Sitti is one of the clearest examples in the setting of idealism curdling into monstrosity.
She first became known as a healer and medical researcher who genuinely sought to ease
suffering. That public image was not wholly false. She once
wanted to help people, and that her descent began not in abstract evil, but in grief.

The turning point was the loss of her twin sister, the original Heart/Intellect OpenSky.
Unable to accept death, Sitti crossed ethical and magical boundaries in an attempt to save
her. When that failed, she went further still: forcing her sister's Prism to pass to her,
erasing the truth, and taking up her sister's place in the world. From that moment on,
Sitti's work stopped being healing in any ordinary sense. It became a campaign against
mortality itself.

She now frames any atrocity as justified if it advances the end of death. That conviction is
what makes her dangerous. Sitti does not see herself as a villain. She sees herself as the
only person willing to do what mercy supposedly requires.

## Additional Details

- Originally presented to the world as a compassionate healer before her ideological turn.
- Her identity is entangled with that of her deceased twin sister, whose legacy she has both
  inherited and corrupted.
- Works best as a villain who believes she is pursuing the greatest possible good.
