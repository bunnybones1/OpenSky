# Ada, the Guardian

![Ada, the Guardian](../images/characters/ada.png)

- Prism: Strength
- Quote: "Someone needs to save the day."
- Age: 32 | Height: 1.85 | Weight: 80kg
- Personality: Determined and Just. Tough but with a soft side for those who need a protector.
- Motivation: End injustice, protect the weak and the Sky.
- Relationship: She does her best to work with others and maintain good relationships with other OpenSkys. Formerly had a relationship with Fox when they were younger, leaving some remaining tension. Was also like family to Horik, but the death of his son has strained this bond.

### Backstory

Ada is part of the race of humans known as The Nordur, who live near the frozen north of Sky, And are renowned for their natural toughness and ability as warriors.

In contrast to who she is today, Ada was born frail, with a weak constitution. However, her spirit proved anything but fragile. From a young age, She strove to protect other children in her village from those who would pick on them, even when her opponents were more extensive and stronger than her, and did all she could to help those in need. She also worked relentlessly to overcome her physical weakness, training every day despite the chiding of children and the concerns of the adults in the village.

This attracted the attention of Horik, the Village's greatest hunter and warrior. Taking Ada under his wing, he helped train her and served almost like a father or older brother. Gradually, Ada's physical strength and health improved, and as she reached adulthood, she became the strongest in the village, able to keep pace with even Horik, who had since become chief.

During this time, the 'Glitch' had begun to pose a threat to their village. During a routine patrol, Ada and her patrol were attacked by a warped, mutated Maw Worm, severely injuring several of them. Ordering the others to retreat, Ada fought the monster alone, endeavouring to buy them time to escape, sustaining grievous injuries during the fight. However, even as her physical strength reached its limit, she refused to back down, eventually triumphing. However, no sooner had it fallen than it lurched back to life, reanimated by some unknown force. Just as it seemed confident Ada would be killed, the Strength Prism reached out to her, drawn to her selfless and unbreakable spirit. Filled with renewed vigour, Ada managed to overpower the beast and force it over a cliff and into the endless Sky below.

Upon returning, Ada was heralded as an even greater hero, now a OpenSky, which her people considered a sacred blessing. However, as time passed, Ada found she was no longer content with protecting her village. Questions filled her mind: "How many others require help but have no one to protect them." This burning call to help others led her to consult with Horik, and eventually, she reluctantly parted ways with her village, travelling out into the world beyond, determined to protect everyone in Sky that needed help, but promising to return home whenever she could.

### Additional Details

- Ada is part of the same human group as Horik, a somewhat Norse-inspired people who live in the colder regions of the Sky and have snowy white hair.
- There could be an interesting link between these people and the Oni Race, as both have white hair regardless of age.
- Ada has a history with Horik. He was like a father/mentor figure to her, being around 20+ years older than her, and he helped to train her as a young child.
- This can then help create stakes and drama in Horik's situation since he is trying to shut out everyone in his quest to get his son back. Most people oblige him, but Ada knows that Horik will destroy himself in pursuit of it and thus wants to protect him.
- Ada was born a weak child with a poor constitution and was disregarded by many other children her age. Despite this, she frequently defended others, even against those far stronger than herself. However, she worked every day to become stronger; eventually, she could stand toe to toe and even above her peers.
- When she was around 20, Ada and a number of the village's defenders were scouting the borders of their territory, trying to find the source of strange storms that had begun to threaten their homes, when they were suddenly attacked by a strange, warped beast, which overpowered the company almost instantly, causing severe casualties among them, and which seemed to shrug off any blow dealt to it. Ada assaulted the monster head-on to create an opening for the wounded to fall back, blocking its path and buying time. Though she suffered countless wounds and it seemed confident she would be killed, Ada refused to back down or retreat, even for a moment. This show of immense strength in defence of others caused the Strength Prism to call out to Ada, choosing her as its OpenSky. Filled with renewed vigour, Ada managed to topple and push back the threat, knocking it over a cliff and into the endless Sky below.
- Ada's Village was amazed at what had happened and greeted her with open arms. Horik, who had become a OpenSky at a similar age to Ada, was incredibly proud.
- Ada continued to defend the village, but felt unsatisfied with the task, feeling a pull to venture into the larger world. Because of her and Horik, their village was safe, but what of the rest of Sky, the countless individuals who had no one to rely on? Eventually, the call became so intense that she decided to leave the village and head into the world to help others.
- Horik was somewhat hesitant for Ada to leave since their combined strengths helped protect the village better, but he was proud of her determination and, as such, encouraged her to follow her calling.
- Several years after Ada left, the worst happened. The "Glitch Storm" struck the village directly, and while Horik was able to protect nearly everyone, even without Ada, in the final moments of the storm, it struck Horik's son as he tried to pull him to safety, killing him and destroying Horik's left arm. Hearing this, Ada would return, finding Horik and the village shaken by the loss.
- In the months that followed, Horik and Ada rebuilt the village and tended to its wounds, but the wounds within Horik never healed. Once things were more or less stable again, Horik stepped down as Chief, feeling he had failed to defend the village. He gave the mantle to another worthy leader and headed off on his own.
- Ada tried to reason with Horik, but he could not be swayed. As he left, Ada realized that she felt the larger world calling to her, but rather than a call of vengeance or loss as Horik felt, it was a call for help. Thanks to Horik's efforts, his son was the only person lost to this most recent disaster, but how many outside their own village suffered without anyone to defend them? As a result, though she stayed in the village for a time, Ada eventually left once more, to continue her quest to protect all those within Sky.
