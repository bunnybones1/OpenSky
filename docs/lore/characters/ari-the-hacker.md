### Backstory

Ari is the youngest of the OpenSkys, having only joined their ranks around a year ago, but he doesn't let that slow him down. Self-confident and sufficient to a fault, He is determined to utilize his vast intellect and newfound power to advance Sky's development and aid all its people. Ari is currently researching the legends of the Genesis Prism, Aiming to locate it and harness its power to improve Sky for everyone, leading him to seek the wisdom of the Fallen Mercenary OpenSky, Axel. His ambition has caused him pain in the past, however, as an accident caused the burn over his left eye. His uncle, Banjo, serves as a mentor and confidant to Ari and has tried to impart the importance of tempering his ambition with caution but with little success.
