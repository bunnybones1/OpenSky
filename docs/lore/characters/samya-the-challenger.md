- Prism: Agility
- Quote: "I'll reach the horizon, and what lies beyond."
- Age: 24 | Height: 1.62m | Weight: 51kg
- Personality: Adventurous Adrenaline-Seeker, Wild-teen
- Motivation: Levity and fun and to break her records.
- Relationship: She is friends with Ada and wants her to loosen up a little. She is also friends with Banjo and admires his carefree spirit. The two like to joke around.

### Backstory

Samya is a superstar speeder, constantly breaking world records in Skyslinging, the famous radical acrobatic sport in which people compete to race through the most extreme conditions and dangerous locations of Sky using an A.R.M. Gear (Acrobatic Rapid Movement).

Samya's skill and fearlessness allowed her to win three consecutive gold medals and become the youngest person in Sky to win more than one gold medal at the age of 18, making her a Sky-wide sensation.

Eventually, her greedy sponsors wanted her to fake a match to make their new up-and-coming Slinger look better. She refused, and her sponsors sabotaged her right ARM gear in secret, which caused it to break during the race. She managed to keep pace using only one ARM gear, but the extra strain on it caused it to break, causing her to fall to what would have been her death. At that moment, however, her commitment to speed and unwillingness to be bought by authority had attracted the Agility Prism to her, and it reactivated her sabotaged right ARM gear, allowing her to avoid death and stay in the race narrowly. People witnessed her remarkable moment of resilience, and despite all the disadvantages, she won the competition, proving to be the fastest and most skilled woman in the whole Sky. That night, Samya would win more than a medal and fans. Her force of will is fully connected with the Agility Prism, The ancient ethereal device that allows people to weave the Sky, allowing her to become a OpenSky.

After this, Samya cut all ties to the institution that had betrayed her and became an independent SkySlinger, promising to push her limits for fame and to be a better OpenSky and protector of Sky.
