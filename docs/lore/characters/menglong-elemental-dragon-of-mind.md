# Meng'Long, Elemental Dragon of Mind

![Meng'Long, Elemental Dragon of Mind](../images/characters/menglong.png)

### The shaper of dreams

- Quote: "We think, and therefore, she is." - Lotus
- Age: Eternally old and young
- Personality: Whimsical, curious, serene, and impossible to pin down.
- Motivation: Curiosity, conversation, and the pursuit of strange understanding.
- Relationship: Generally well-disposed toward mortals and dragons alike, though often puzzling to both.

## Backstory

Meng'Long is one of the most paradoxical of the dragons. Associated with mind, dreams,
and mutable form, she seems to move through reality with the same fluidity that others
move through thought. She is peaceful, elusive, and
profoundly difficult to understand even by draconic standards.

She is said to remember things from before her own birth into Sky, which makes her both
older and younger than she should be. That contradiction defines her presence. She asks
questions instead of giving answers, speaks in riddles, and seems less interested in ruling
than in provoking insight.

## Additional Details

- Strongly associated with the moon, night, and the Dreamfield.
- Can freely reshape her own form.
- Often leaves the Dreamfield to satisfy her curiosity.
- People who seek direct answers from her tend to receive puzzles instead, but often find
  wisdom in them later.
