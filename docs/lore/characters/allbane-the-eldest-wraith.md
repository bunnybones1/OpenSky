# Allbane, the Eldest Wraith

![Allbane, The Eldest Wraith](../images/characters/albane.png)

- Quote: "Life and death is an endless cycle. One I help to keep moving."
- Stature: Towering even by wraith standards, standing roughly 7 feet tall.
- Personality: Sardonic, cynical, observant, and unexpectedly patient.
- Motivation: To feed, to observe, and to maintain the cycle as he understands it.
- Relationship: Surrogate parent, teacher, and oldest confidant of Bouran.

## Backstory

Allbane is one of the eldest and most unsettling of the wraiths: an ancient predator whose
relationship to life and death is both intimate and unnervingly casual. He is feared by
most beings on instinct alone, yet the source gives him a more complicated role than that
of a simple monster.

That complication begins with Bouran. Instead of consuming the child he found alone in
the Undercroft, Allbane chose to raise them. Whether out of curiosity, recognition, or
some deeper philosophy, he became teacher, guardian, and witness to Bouran's growth.
That choice turns Allbane into one of the setting's more unusual figures: a being of death
who, in his own alien way, preserves as much as he devours.

## Additional Details

- One of the eldest wraiths.
- Sees life and death as movement within a larger order, not as moral absolutes.
- Works well as a figure who is protective without ever becoming warm or conventional.
