# Banjo, the Cubeman

![Banjo, the Cubeman](../images/characters/banjo.png)

- Prism: Wisdom + Intellect
- Stature: Broad-shouldered and solidly built, around 1.88m tall.
- Personality: Benign, mysterious, playful, and trickster-like.
- Motivation: Banjo appears to interfere in events to preserve a larger balance, even if he rarely explains why.
- Relationship: Uncle of Ari and a guardian-like figure to Mai and Mira.

### Backstory

Banjo reads like a wandering cosmic meddler: part sage, part comedian, part
unpredictable force of correction. He moves through the world as a troublemaker and
guide, nudging people in unexpected directions and appearing wherever the story seems
to need a hand on the scales.

Those around him rarely know how seriously to take him. Banjo jokes, breaks tension,
and behaves with the casual irreverence of someone who knows more than he is willing
to explain. But beneath the nonsense is a pattern: he repeatedly appears in moments
where people need to survive, escape, or grow. That makes him less a random eccentric
than a deeply unreliable-seeming steward of balance.

### Additional Details

- Travels widely and often influences events in strange ways to preserve balance.
- Carries some fourth-wall-breaking, tutorial-guide energy in the source notes.
- Tutorial Cube is described as an old friend of Banjo's.
- No one seems to know how old he really is.
- He may be one of the oldest OpenSkys, possibly even the first.
- He serves as a godfather-like protector to Mai and Mira.
