# Axel, the Mercenary

![Axel, the Mercenary](../images/characters/axel.png)

- Prism: Wisdom + Heart
- Quote: "The Only Certainty in Life is its Ending."
- Age: 38 | Height: 1.80m | Weight: 80kg
- Personality: Self-sufficient, Tactically Pragmatic and Cynical.
- Motivation: Vengeance Upon Sitti, Completion of his contracts.
- Relationship: He holds nothing but coldness and indifference for other OpenSkys, seeing them as naive and blind to the cold truths of the world.

### Backstory

Born with the rare ability of precognition, Axel once sought to use his ability to see the future to help others, warning communities of future disasters like earthquakes, typhoons, and other events he foresaw. However, as time went on, his visions became more vivid, relating not to large-scale events but to the destinies of individuals. Try as he might, Axel found himself unable to shift or change the oncoming deaths and suffering of those around him and became despondent, now seeing his gift as a curse, and seeking out someone who could free him of it, finally meeting Sitti, who offered her help. However, Axel awoke only into greater despair, finding that not only had Sitti stolen his eyes but that in the darkness of his mind, the visions burned more brightly than ever.

This torment led to Axel eventually adopting a nihilistic view of the world, seeing his visions as the will of fate itself, and choosing to take up the self-serving mantle of a mercenary, separating himself from others and adopting a sarcastic, sardonic exterior, regarding everything with a dark sense of humour. After all, if nothing can be changed, why care - life is a mere stage play. Despite this nearly immovable mask, Axel still longs to be proven wrong - for someone to challenge his visions and prove that fate is not set in stone.
