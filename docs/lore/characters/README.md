# Characters

Named figures of Sky, including OpenSkys, dragons, rivals, and other important beings.

## OpenSkys

- [Ada, the Guardian](./ada-the-guardian.md): A northern protector chosen by Strength who left home to defend anyone in Sky who needs help.
- [Ari, the Hacker](./ari-the-hacker.md): A prodigy OpenSky who wants to improve Sky through intellect, invention, and the search for the Genesis Prism.
- [Axel, the Mercenary](./axel-the-mercenary.md): A fallen seer whose gift for prophecy became a curse and drove him toward despair.
- [Bouran, the Shadow](./bouran-the-shadow.md): Bouran is a Heart-aligned OpenSky shaped by the darkest regions of Sky rather than by civil society.
- [Fox, the Vagabond](./fox-the-vagabond.md): A runaway prince of Armis who chose freedom over the throne and now wanders Sky as a rogue hero.
- [Horik, the Exile](./horik-the-exile.md): A former leader broken by loss who hunts for the power to reclaim what the Glitch Storm took from him.
- [Iris, the Loreseeker](./iris-the-loreseeker.md): Iris is a scholar-pilgrim whose pursuit of knowledge is inseparable from her search for meaning.
- [Lotus, the Sage](./lotus-the-sage.md): Lotus is the grandmaster of the Great Library of Alcazar and one of the most revered OpenSkys still living.
- [Mai, the Tinkerer](./mai-the-tinkerer.md): A bold inventor from war-torn Armis whose ambition and creativity helped reshape her life in Kore City.
- [Mira, the Mechanist](./mira-the-mechanist.md): Mira's childhood was defined by catastrophe and reconstruction.
- [Samya, the Challenger](./samya-the-challenger.md): A record-breaking skyslinger whose refusal to submit to corruption drew the Agility Prism to her.
- [Sitti, the Warlock](./sitti-the-warlock.md): Sitti is one of the clearest examples in the setting of idealism curdling into monstrosity.
- [Titus, the Hermit](./titus-the-hermit.md): A former crystal harvester who turned against exploitation after the wilderness itself saved his life.
- [Zoey, the Maverick](./zoey-the-maverick.md): Zoey grew up inside Armis under the brutal logic of a state that claimed order while abandoning its weakest citizens.

## Dragons

- [Amaruath, Elemental Dragon of Earth](./amaruath-elemental-dragon-of-earth.md): An ancient earth dragon whose authority is tied to endurance, land, and civilizational memory.
- [Cryogen, Elemental Dragon of Water](./cryogen-elemental-dragon-of-water.md): A water dragon whose fractured rule left the Valkyrn defined by loyalty, collapse, and exile.
- [Dracomantium, Elemental Dragon of Metal](./dracomantium-elemental-dragon-of-metal.md): Youngest dragon and metal guardian, haunted by her failed mortal empire.
- [Gusto, Elemental Dragon of Air](./gusto-elemental-dragon-of-air.md): Gusto is the dragon of freedom in its purest and most dangerous form.
- [Kha Meth, Elemental Dragon of Light](./kha-meth-elemental-dragon-of-light.md): Rashida's sun-dragon, worshipped as a protector and feared as an uncompromising judge.
- [Meng'Long, Elemental Dragon of Mind](./menglong-elemental-dragon-of-mind.md): Meng'Long is one of the most paradoxical of the dragons.
- [Tiamat, Elemental Dragon of Fire](./tiamat-elemental-dragon-of-fire.md): Tiamat embodies the double nature of fire: creation and destruction, inspiration and ruin.
- [Undragon, Elemental Dragon of Dark](./undragon-elemental-dragon-of-dark.md): The Undragon is treated less like a ruler and more like a principle given form.

## Other Figures

- [Allbane, The Eldest Wraith](./allbane-the-eldest-wraith.md): An ancient wraith who raised Bouran and treats life, death, and predation as parts of one cold order.
- [Banjo, the Cubeman](./banjo-the-cubeman.md): Banjo reads like a wandering cosmic meddler: part sage, part comedian, part unpredictable force of correction.
- [Gerry](./gerry.md): A brash zomboid brawler who leads a ragtag gang and treats every problem like the start of a street-fight.
- [King Altren](./king-altren.md): The absent king of Armis whose authority and expectations still define Fox's rebellion.
- [Sitti, the original (deceased)](./sitti-the-original-deceased.md): Sitti's dead twin, remembered as the healer and idealist whose loss set the current Sitti on her darker path.
- [The Arcadeum Mask](./the-arcadeum-mask.md): A haunted mask containing a knowledge-hoarding spirit that can act only through a host.
- [The Bard](./the-bard.md): A chaos-aligned herald of Yxxathoxzz who turns performance into temptation and disorder.
- [The Training Bot](./the-training-bot.md): A training construct that grounds the game's tutorial logic in the world itself.
- [Yxxathoxzz, the Oblivion](./yxxathoxzz-the-oblivion.md): The sleeping devourer at the center of the Dreamfield, both archive and existential threat.
