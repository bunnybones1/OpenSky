# Lotus, the Sage

![Lotus, the Sage](../images/characters/lotus.png)

- Prism: Wisdom
- Age: 102 | Height: 1.60m | Weight: 66kg
- Personality: Calm, enigmatic, and deeply contemplative.
- Motivation: To preserve balance in Sky and keep dangerous power out of reckless hands.
- Relationship: Mentor figure to many younger weavers, with a particularly close bond to Titus.

### Backstory

Lotus is the grandmaster of the Great Library of Alcazar and one of the most revered
OpenSkys still living. The latest source frames him as the only known OpenSky from
the First Era who remains active in the present age, which makes him less a mere scholar
than a living bridge to the oldest surviving traditions of Sky.

His authority does not come from conquest or spectacle, but from patience, restraint, and
perspective. Lotus teaches, meditates, studies, and watches. He is the kind of figure
others seek when they need wisdom rather than force.

That same restraint is what drives his current arc. Lotus has chosen to seek the Genesis
Prism not to wield it for glory, but to keep it out of the hands of those who would misuse
it. Even he appears to doubt whether any individual should possess that kind of power.
