# Fox, the Vagabond

![Fox, the Vagabond](../images/characters/fox.png)

- Prism: Strength + Agility
- Quote: "Don't act like you're not impressed, sweetheart!"
- Age: 32 | Height: 1.90m | Weight: 95kg
- Personality: Overconfident ladies man
- Motivation: Protect people's freedom.
- Relationship: Fox has a past with Ada. Son of King Altren.

### Backstory

Fox is a free-spirit, stylish ronin with a wise-cracking sense of humour and a huge ego. While he can be egotistical and arrogant sometimes, his heart is pure. Fox uses his nickname to hide his past and real identity.

With a childhood built with rules and duties as the only child of the Royal Armis Family and rightful heir to the throne. The prince soon realized he was trapped in the life his family wanted him to have, and his future wouldn't be his own. He believes that the idea of being a ruler goes against what he holds more significant in life: the freedom he never had as a kid; after finishing his royal training and becoming a sword master, he chose not to be responsible for ruling people and squashing their freedom as part of the royal family empire. On the day of his coronation, he vanished into the shadows. Never to be found. He would never go by Giacomo Armissilianni VII again, assuming only the nickname "Fox." He was finally independent, as his people. His choice of abdicating for the throne would awaken the Strength-Agility Prism, making him a skilled OpenSky protector of Sky.

Now, Fox lives as a wanderer, constantly travelling the Sky roads with no home but the horizon. He is finally free.

### Additional Details

- He assumes the nickname "Fox" because, as a kid, he saw a fox once in the forest and wondered why that Fox was free to do what it wanted, and he wasn't.
- Ada is the only person in the Sky with whom Fox shares his past and real name.
- There might be a plot where when Fox Revisits Armis, he learns that his old city is in chaos with no leadership. Multiple generals and advisors are all vying for the throne.
