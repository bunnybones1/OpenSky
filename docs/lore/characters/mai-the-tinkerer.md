# Mai, the Tinkerer

![Mai, the Tinkerer](../images/characters/mai.png)

- Prism: Agility + Intellect
- Quote: "Kawaii!"
- Age: 19 | Height: 1.55m | Weight: 47kg
- Personality: Friendly Cheerful Geek.
- Motivation: Beat Mira.
- Relationship: Rivalry with Mira.

### Backstory

Mai, a refugee from the war-torn region of Armis, found a new purpose in Nexus/Core City alongside her companions Ari, Mira, and Banjo. Together, they transformed their survival story into a quest for greatness within the Arena they established, symbolizing resilience and hope. As the group's strategist, Mai's ambition to become the supreme OpenSky wasn't just for personal glory but to uplift their community. Her journey to find the Genesis Prism with Mira evolved from a friendly rivalry into a shared mission for a brighter future, demonstrating their growth from refugees to key figures in their city's revival.

### Additional Details

- Mai and Mira's Mother, or mentoring figure, Althea, was Sky's greatest inventor. She built the Arena and the Soul Forge. She built the soul forge for Sitti without knowing what it would do when powered by the ether crystals, but upon finding out it was a perversion of the cycle, she cut ties with Sitti and wandered the land as a healer, repenting for her mistake.
- She was respected by everyone and praised for her unrivalled ingenuity, creativity, and unrequited love for all life.
- Althea was Sitti's sister and lifelong friend of Banjo.
- Althea and Sitti were always best friends until Sitti perverted the cycle.
- When Althea returns to Kore City, she is thrust into motherhood. Banjo, a hagrid-type character, appeals to her empathy and asks her to take care of two child refugees from the war caused by Armis.
