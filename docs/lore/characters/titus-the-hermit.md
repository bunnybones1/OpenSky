# Titus, the Hermit

![Titus, the Hermit](../images/characters/titus.png)

- Prism: Strength + Wisdom
- Quote: "Keep the beat, follow the melody."
- Age: 34 | Height: 2.10m | Weight: 137kg
- Personality: Gentle hippie with a spirited environmentalist soul.
- Motivation: Protect and preserve the natural world.
- Relationship: He is distrusting of humans and prefers to spend time with his friends from nature. He can speak to plants and animals.

### Backstory

Titus used to work harvesting Ether Crystals from the Forests of Sky. He only thought of his work to provide ether energy to the people of Sky until one day, a digging mecha accidentally struck him and fell deep into the woods. Stranded alone for days, with his leg shattered and away from his team, he would have died, but the creatures of the forest took him in and nursed him back to health, calling his entire worldview into question. Titus lived among them for weeks and realized the incredible life the forest held, endangered by the harvester's exploitation.

After healing their human guest, the creatures revealed the source of ether crystals, the 'World Tree,' at the center of Etherwald, which is said to be the soul of Ethera herself. Restored, Titus would return to his fellow harvesters, who had assumed he was dead after so long. He tried to impart his newfound respect for nature and advise them to leave the forest alone, but they brushed him off. Titus also accidentally revealed the existence of the World Tree to the harvesters. Seeing this as the ultimate payoff, his former allies led a direct assault against the World Tree.

Betrayed, Titus fought alongside the forest against his old colleagues. His determination and respect for the environment made the Wisdom/Strength prism appear to him, giving him the power to force back the giant mechs and drive them away from Etherwald for good. Since then, Titus has found a new purpose in life. He has vowed to walk the horizon with his old guitar and to protect the natural world wherever it is threatened. Titus prefers the company of animals and plants to people, but in his heart, he believes he will be able to make people aware of the value and importance of nature.
