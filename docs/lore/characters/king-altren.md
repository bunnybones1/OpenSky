# King Altren

![King Altren](../images/characters/kingaltren.png)

King Altren is the absent center of Fox's story: the ruler of Armis, the father Fox walked
away from, and one of the clearest embodiments of duty as burden rather than honor.

## Narrative Role

Altren matters less as an active adventurer than as the weight of inheritance made personal.
He represents the version of power Fox refused: lineage, obligation, ceremony, and the
belief that a life can belong to a state before it belongs to the person living it.

## Place In Armis

As king, Altren stands for the continuity of Armis itself. Whether he is remembered as a
steady ruler, a hard ruler, or simply a necessary one, his presence helps explain why Fox's
flight was not a private family dispute but a political rupture with consequences for the
whole empire.
