# The Glitch Storm

The Glitch Storm is one of Sky's main engines for crisis stories. Where the world reference explains what it is, this narrative entry focuses on what it does to a story.

## Narrative Use

- It creates sudden disaster without needing a conventional invading army.
- It can transform geography, creatures, and civilians in ways that force characters into moral triage.
- It destabilizes borders and history, making it useful for refugee stories, survival arcs, and regional collapse.
- It lets a story pivot quickly from local conflict to existential threat.

## Story Pressure

The Glitch Storm works best when it is more than background weather. It should force choices about what can be saved, who gets protected first, and whether knowledge, power, or sacrifice is the only way through it.
