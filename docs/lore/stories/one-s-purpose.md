# One's purpose

"What's your name?" - asked the boy.

"Name? A word by which a person or thing is known." - The Servitor paused briefly. "One does not need a name."

"Well, I need to know who I should thank! You saved me!" - The kid happily replied. The boy appeared to be no more than ten. His hair was white and curly, and he wore nothing but rags, except a golden medallion around his neck.

"...Saved?" Asked the mysterious armored angel. "One only did what was meant to be done, nothing more, nothing less. For a long time, one was not aware of its existence. Only recently, something happened, and one is starting to have thoughts of its own." The angel sat upon the shore and cleaned the sand off his hands. By his side lay the still body of a giant kraken, which had attempted to prey upon the boy.

"Saving is simply one's duty. Something that one must do for those in danger."

"Okay, still... Thank you, Angelo!" - The child smiled.

"Angelo?" When the winged Servitor heard that name, a surge of memories flooded his mind. The boy was not the first one to call him by that name. How could he know about this?

The shallow waves touched his foot again, and he contemplated the sunset on the horizon. Something was wrong. He couldn't remember how he arrived here, this small island in the middle of the ocean. What was his last action? He was not even sure if he was the one who had killed the giant Kraken...

"How... How did we get here?" - He asked the boy.

"I don't know. You created this place."

"Is this a dream?"

"You have been dreaming for ages, constantly reliving this moment, trying to understand what you've done wrong and why you couldn't do what you were supposed to do, why you couldn't save me."

Slowly, the old Servitor started to remember. This child was nothing but a memory of a time long forgotten-a time when the Sky was still young, and few existed. His role was to protect and serve the Sky's firstborns. He failed his purpose.

The Servitor realized he was never on an island but buried deep in the ocean. His wings are chained, and his legs and arms are fossilized inside coral reefs. He has been there for generations, lying alongside the scattered bones of a Kraken and holding a golden medallion in its giant jaws.

At last, the memories that clouded Angelo's mind began to fade. He looked up and noticed that a team of Turtle-like humanoids was gathered around him. They seemed like scientists, wearing high-tech outfits and carrying laboratory gadgets. They were studying him.

Angelo starts moving and notices several cables attached to his head, seemingly connected as though to extract something from his mind. As he awakes, the Tortugan in front of him appears uneasy and afraid.

"What is this? Where am I?"

"You are in the Forgotten Ocean." - A female tortugan replied.

"Who are you?"

"I'm Lanna, a junior member of Tortugan explorers and..." Before the young Tortugan could finish her sentence, she was interrupted and pushed aside by an older one. He was big and fat, with an eye patch. Some of his limbs appeared mechanical. He approached the face of the chained angel. -"Brr! This one seems defective. Why was he talking to himself?" - He moved his hand, which ended in a hook, close to Angelo's face. - "Now, you'll tell us what we want to know, Servitor. That's your program. That's your purpose."

"Please, sir... I think this one is different; let me talk with him. It looks like he is aware of us, this is unprecedented!"

"We don't have time for this! A Kraken graveyard is not a safe place to stay! Extract the information from his databanks now! OPEN IT! Open his head! We need to know the secret of the Qui!"

Suddenly, Angelo propelled his body up, easily tearing through the chains around him. The Algae covering his armour was quickly carried away by the ocean current. He moved his hand up, pointing to the pirate scientist whose eyes had grown wide and filled with fear.

"No." Angelo closed his hands, creating a burst of energy that immediately fragmented all the high-tech gadgets around him. It was like he could manipulate things with his mind on a subatomic level.

The Tortugan team was dumbfounded, stripped bare, seeing all their pieces of technology scattered into parts flowing slowly in the water.

Angelo looked up at the weak beam of light coming from the surface. He opened his wings wide and took flight toward the Sky. The force of his takeoff was so strong that it propelled everything away, creating a small crater.

He emerged from the water, flying straight up into the clouds at breakneck speed, before suddenly coming to a stop. He held the golden medallion in his hands and stared at the sunset. He knew what he needed to do next.

"I will find you, brothers. And as I did, you shall awake."
