# Of Light and Darkness

"Oh Eternal Sun, please forgive my intrusion." the priestess spoke, prostrating before the dragon-god as he sat, coiled about his throne.

"Arise, child. The light bids you well. For what reason have you sought my counsel?" Kha Meht's voice rumbled, a soft sound, like distant thunder.

The priestess rose slightly, but remained in a kneeling position, her head bowed.

"A strange outsider has come to Rashida. They demanded an audience with you. We have attempted to dissuade them, but..."

As if on cue, a hooded figure appeared behind the priestess in the temple's archway, silhouetted against the brilliant midday sun. The priestess looked back in stunned horror as the hooded figure approached, striding past her and towards Kha Meht without a hint of hesitation or fear.

Kha Mhet descended slightly from the ornate throne he resided upon, serpentine body sliding across the polished sandstone floor, regarding the stranger with a bemused expression.

"How dare you approach our lord so!" the priestess spoke indignantly. "Show some proper respect and kneel..."

"Leave us, Vishiva. Return to the temple. It's nearly time for the evening rites." Kha Meht spoke softly but firmly to her.

Dumbstruck, Vishiva simply bowed her head in acknowledgement and backed out, leaving the two alone.

"You are a bold one, to approach me directly. I commend your spirit, child."

"Bold?" The stranger replied "Should I fear speaking with one of Sky's Guardians? Your duty is to protect this world and its people, is it not?"

The dragon smiled, amused at the stranger's impudence. "It is indeed, but most would consider that something worthy of respect and fear."

"It certainly seems that you have made it so. Your followers do seem set upon adoring and fearing you."

Kha Meht drew closer to the figure, towering over them.

"I have provided Rashida with life, light, and prosperity beyond its years. Is that not an act worthy of their respect and adoration?"

"I did not mean to offend" the figure replied, bowing their head slightly "I sought you out to request something of you."

Kha Meht relaxed slightly, "and what would that be, my child?"

"Cease your people's assaults upon the Undercroft and the creatures of the Dark. They have done nothing to provoke such actions from Rashida."

Kha Meht bristled, but remained calm "As a Guardian of Rashida and the Sky, It is my duty to protect its people. The Dark poses a threat to us all."

"And what of those who are born of Darkness, who rely on it to live?"

"Sacrifices must be made for the greater good." Kha Meht spoke tersely.

"Greater Good according to whom? You? Light and Darkness are both part of The Cycle. They depend on one another to exist. Surely you cannot be blind to this fact." The figure replied.

"Who are you, to speak to me of The Cycle? I was there at its birth, I am the Firstborn of Sky." Kha Meht growled, eyes flickering like burning coals.

"I am merely the messenger of its will. It has been quite clear on this. Your unjust crusade against the Undercroft, and those within it, must cease."

Kha Mhet slid forward and began circling the hooded figure, coiling around them while keeping his eyes fixed on their form. Though the figure did not move, he felt as though their eyes stared back at him from beneath the darkness of their hood.

"We Dragons are tasked with protecting all of Sky. You have no right to tell me how I can or cannot protect my people." Kha Meht hissed.

"Ah, I see. So are you a protector of Sky, or just those people you consider to be worthy of being 'yours?'" the figure replied, calm as ever.

Arcs of lightning crackled in Kha Mhet's mouth as he hissed his reply.

"Darkness is not under my protection. It is Disparate from Sky. A mistake to be snuffed out. We dragons do not serve it."

The figure seemed to smile as it replied "Oh? But one among you does. How quickly you seem to have forgotten your eldest sibling, Undra..."

All his patience suddenly lost, Kha Meht's immediate, thunderous reply shook the very foundations of the temple. He tightened his coils inward, trapping the figure, nearly squeezing the life from them.

"DO NOT SPEAK OF THAT 'THING!' IT IS AN ABOMINATION! A BLIGHT UPON Sky ITSELF! IT IS NOT ONE OF US! TO EVEN UTTER ITS NAME IS SACRILEGE!" The stones themselves rattled at his voice, streams of sand trickling down from above as the whole temple trembled.

Suddenly, the figure's body became shadowy, as though made of mist, slipping effortlessly from Kha Meht's grasp, sliding along the floor and up the wall to a small window high upon the temple wall.

"It seems that I touched a nerve." They replied with a small smirk.

"I won't press any further, but know this. If you or your people unlawfully attack the Undercroft again, I will not hesitate to... 'intervene.'" The figure opened their cloak slightly, revealing a pair of huge purple scythes hanging from their belt.

"Good day then, 'First of the Living.' May The Cycle bless you."

An instant later, they hopped from the ledge and were gone, and an instant after that, so was a sizable chunk of the temple wall, atomized as Kha Meht's breath swept over the place where the figure had stood a moment before.

"And you as well..." Kha Meht hissed as Vishiva and some of his guards rushed back in, worried by the commotion. "Because if you ever show your face here again, I will ensure that you are returned to it."
