# The Prisoner

"What will you do now that we've captured you, thief?" the guard asked, carrying the man in handcuffs into his cell.

"I'll enjoy my brief visit here." Tatt replied.

"Brief visit? This is the biggest fortress prison in all of Sky. No one has escaped from here in over five hundred years." - the guard steps away and activates the laser grids, locking the cell.

"Then I must say that you will have a surprise at dawn."

"Haha, you don't seem crazy to me. Maybe you're just deluded?" - the guard pulls a chair and sits down, taking off his helmet and revealing a skeptical smile. - Alright, you have my attention!

"Yes, we are warriors created under the blazing sun, and our strength comes from our devotion and faith in Light."

"I have no doubt. But in this fantasy, even after you manage to beat me, to escape from here, you would still have to face the twin Gemini that guards the prison. And personally, those metal sentinels are one of the few things that scare me around here. So tell me, Tatt, how would you do such a feat?"

"Sleeping."

"Haha, I take it back. You ARE insane! I suppose you'll just sleep-walk, your way out, then?"

"Not quite, but sleep allows you to go places if you know how to dream." The man continued, "You see, the Dreamfields is vast and knows no boundaries." "The laws of reality known to us do not apply there. So I won't need to hack the laser grids. Instead, I will pass right through them. I won't have to fight you. I will dream of my homeland, and the Masked Dreamers will take me there.

"Masked Dreamers? They're just a children's fairytale! How can you believe in such nonsense?"

"Indeed. You will see that children are far wiser than you or I. Their lack of preconceptions about the world makes them genuinely free spirits."

"Tell me, guard, when did you stop dreaming? When did you put boundaries upon your creativity and ground your mind in common sense? I'm not the one trapped in a cage, my friend."

"Alright, Rashidan, I guess I won't see you tomorrow then?" - the guard muttered skeptically.

"I guess you won't." - Tatt smiled, closing his eyes.
